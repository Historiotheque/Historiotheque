# Changelog

All notable changes to The Historiotheque — the practice itself, versioned as
cultural software — are recorded here, newest first. The format follows
*Keep a Changelog*: one entry per published version, written by hand, for
human readers.

**Versioning note.** This changelog records releases *of the practice itself*.
The repositories of the Historiotheque organization on GitHub carry their own
version numbers, and their release notes live with their repos — a separate
lineage, never merged with this one. Before September 2026, The Historiotheque
was not a running software system: its releases were public announcements
(published on Medium) of the state of a documented art-research practice,
versioned as cultural software. The GitHub era begins with the entries under
*Unreleased* below.

**Reconstruction note.** Entries from 2.0.0 to 5.1.0 were compiled on
2026-10-01 from the published Official Release texts; dates and descriptions
follow those texts, including their own published corrections. In the earliest
releases, the version numbers of The Historiotheque and of projects housed
within it were occasionally entangled — the releases themselves document the
corrections. From the next release onward, entries are written at release time.

## [5.1.0] — 2026-07-03

MINOR VERSION — backwards-compatible with 5.0.0.

### Added

- The Delta-Workspace Engine — the workspace as a dynamic, calculation-ready
  surface (workspace manifolds, topological trajectories).
- Atmospheric Tone Field Pipeline — daily field recordings paired with tonal
  tableaux.
- Open Science Framework (OSF) Integration — a structured multi-media research
  hierarchy for the projects.
- The Substack Broadcast Layer — a formal public channel for Official
  Releases and documentation.
- Archeological Sound Asset Recovery — legacy 1994–1995 audio (THE
  CIRCUS-MACHINE) recovered and synthesis-matched.

### Changed

- Knowledge Management Syntax Optimization — the Refcards schema standardized
  across physical and digital instances.
- Cognitive Strategy Core Realignment — Adversarial Rationality replaces the
  older Algorithmic Rationality module.

## [3.0.5] — 2024-05-01

Preliminary launch — the most prolific period of the operation to that date.
Shut down entirely two days later (IMPORTANT UPDATE, May 3, 2024); hiatus
until further notice.

## [3.0.4] — 2023-11-23

Relaunch and subsequent closing down. The DISMANTLING THE ARCHIVE angle is
developed in this period.

## [3.0.3] — 2023

Relaunched after the March 2023 hyper-productive period; ran a few weeks,
then closed down again.

## [3.0.2] — 2023-03-05

Relaunch after the sabbatical that followed 3.0.1 — a gap of some six years.

## [3.0.1] — 2016-12-29

Stable. Hiatus announced: a sabbatical for most of 2017, spent on data
analysis of the first runs.

## [2.0.3] — 2016

Rebuilt almost entirely from scratch after the failure of 2.0.2. First
formulation of The Historiotheque as something built within the operator as
much as in the physical workspace.

## [2.0.2] — 2016

Released, and failed; the operation closed until further notice.

## [2.0.1] — 2016-08-20

The Historiotheque as a vision of the perfect workspace for a contemporary
interdisciplinary artist. Shut down the following day; Production-Year
2017–2018 closed in a little over a day.

## [2.0.0] — 2016-05

Launched earlier in the year in the new location (end of April / beginning
of May).
