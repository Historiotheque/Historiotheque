# Projects

The projects of the Art Operation — the actual art-research practice, listed as
projects. This index replaces an earlier version that listed the repositories;
the repositories are containers (they are listed in the organisation profile),
not the projects themselves. The works are these.

Project charters live in this folder, one per project, written as each project
is taken up. A charter is the project's own declaration, in the first person:
what the project is, when it opened, what it has made, and what it draws on.

## The projects

- **The Historiotheque** — the core operational studio and interdisciplinary
  research laboratory; a platform for cultural software, archival research,
  and the integration of artwork and field studies. This repository is its home.
- **The History-Project** — an interdisciplinary art-research project (opened
  July 15, 2001; main run to October 2004; dormant, reopenable): images, sounds,
  and words — essays, fiction and non-fiction — around Quebec history, the
  seigneurial system, survivance, and historical therapy. The novel of the same
  name, in the ExperimentalNovel repository, grew out of this project; the
  project is the larger thing. Charter: below.
- **The Revolt of Fiction** — a metafictional literary trilogy exploring
  narrative structures, post-archival concepts, and polyphonic fiction:
  *The History-Project* (Historiogenesis), *The Archives-Project*
  (Archivogenesis), *The Chronotopium* (Algorithmogenesis).
- **The Deep-Archives Project** — the long-term custodianship, repair, and
  study of large digital archives: my own, and another artist's — some three
  terabytes that came into my keeping — with the systems, scripts, and cold
  storage the work requires. Its question is how a personal archive is kept
  against entropy, so that it can be transmitted.
- **ANATOMY OF AN AESTHETIC PROBLEM** — an art-research project featuring the
  nested mixed-media painting series THE ITERATED EVOLUTION OF AN IDEA,
  examining the development and transformation of concepts through visual media.
- **The Chronotopium-Series** — mixed-media analog paintings, nested inside
  the Chronotopium-Project.
- **NOISE FIELDS** — abstract digital paintings: mostly random images made
  with heavy pixelation and noisy distributions of pixel values.
- **ATMOSPHERIC TONE** — an ongoing field-art series capturing daily ambient
  environmental audio, combined with still images for publication as visual
  soundscapes.
- **Symphonie du Vieux Village** — an open-ended, asynchronous symphonic
  soundscape project: ten years of deep-time sonic exploration and residence
  in Mont-Saint-Hilaire.
- **The Painter's Almanach** — a multi-year, seasonal art-research framework
  structured around an autumn-to-summer cycle, to discipline and track
  artistic output.
- **The Refcards-System / Refmats** — an information-management methodology
  using physical index cards, custom software, and a faceted taxonomy to
  organize research topics and workspace materials.
- **Schizobot** — an original coding project: a machine intelligence meant to
  replace me as an artist, ironically — "the stupidest system in the universe."
  Schizobot Lite (2026) is its smaller, streamlined successor.
- **A collage series (unnamed)** — a prospective painting series in collage,
  analog and digital; not yet named.
