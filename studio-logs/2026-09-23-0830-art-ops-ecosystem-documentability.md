## What I did

Expanded the research arc from one question to five. Formulated RQ-2026-014
(the 1907–1914 operation's workspace/workflow vs. the contemporary institutional
production of Picasso knowledge — the Picasso Administration, archives,
Succession/rights regime — as Operation B; asymmetry: operation A documented
works but not procedures, operation B documents everything *about* the works).

Formulated RQ-2026-015 (operation-to-operation interfaces across time and space:
synchronic — Kahnweiler ↔ Apollinaire/Salmon ↔ Section d'Or ↔ Steins; diachronic —
the 1973 fork, discourse as commit history) and designated it the **bridge**: it
stays in WhatIsCubism and *ends* it, launching the ArtOps Ecosystem mini-project.

Took in the 2015 ArtOps document (art operations as DAOs/automata/finite state
machines; broker_ops as art-market intermediaries; ArtExchangeOps; originated for my
novels, 2024 AI-voiced elaboration filed as elaboration not origin) and formulated
RQ-2026-016, the ecosystem project's founding question: what a designed, partly
autonomous ecosystem of art operations looks like, and who the artist is when automata
make the art.

Compared Picasso's and Warhol's documentation practices in art-operation terms:
Picasso (object-centric: dated works, kept everything, no procedural records) vs.
Warhol (Time Capsules 610 boxes from 1974, daily diaries 1976–87, Polaroid Big Shot
tens of thousands of exposures, tape recorder) — Warhol more systematic, still
surface/traces rather than procedures. **Documentability is non-monotonic with time:**
Renaissance guilds (contracts, account books) > modern genius (mystique, procedural
collapse) > Warhol (partial recovery) > the Git era. Formulated RQ-2026-017 on that
basis: the documentability of art operations across history and what the New
Documentation must do for art operators today.

Analyzed the Global Research Schema + Annotated Taxonomy: Taxonomy = the territory,
GRS = the coordinate system, my schema = the engine. Drafted the 12-folder Research
registry snapshot (added `sound` after ingesting my ~150 Academia.edu interests —
the Taxonomy's blind spot).
