## Decisions

**Decision:** Workspace temperature is derived, not declared; its form is three QoT qualia (Option 2), no hue or Kelvin terms.
**Reason:** I forget to declare things; a declared field would sit empty. The record holds the deltas; the qualia carry the reading in my own idiom, as they did on the moraltempcards.

**Decision:** Frontmatter time becomes `sessions:` — derived clusters (60-minute silence gap; ≥3 traces or ~10 minutes), ±15-minute stated tolerance, ratified by me at drafting; `date:` is the first session's date.
**Reason:** time_start/time_end falsified multi-session arcs — the 0908 log's frontmatter claimed a session that never happened. The frontmatter must add information, never dress an envelope as a session.

**Decision:** Segment headers are minted only at configuration switches and carry the full span, date then time; sub-threshold switches are narrated as switch texture.
**Reason:** A delta exists only where the configuration changes; the reader must see the global picture of each work session at a glance, and rapid switching must stay visible without shredding the page.

**Decision:** The proposal passes the §9 threshold for a new spec version; the five changes are ratified; v1.7 is minted at publication (2026-10-04), with this log as its first application, and the vocabulary lives beside the spec, born only from ratified logs.
**Reason:** Structural change plus a demonstrated falsehood is exactly what versions are for; vocabulary grows from practice, never ahead of it.

**Decision:** The Switchboard Method introduction is corrected as recorded above, in three amendment rounds, each noted at the top of the document.
**Reason:** The document must describe the practice as it is: rapid irregular switching across several dozen projects, decisionless rather than memoryless, its gains latency removal and avoided deliberation — no absolutes.

**Decision:** "Self-archiving" is not used for this practice; archiving is described as second nature.
**Reason:** The term already means something else in archival science (author self-deposit). The accurate frame is habitual and continuous, automatic for the operator, never automated.
