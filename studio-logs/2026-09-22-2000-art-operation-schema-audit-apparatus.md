## What I did

Formulated the first research question of the self-directed PhD-equivalent coursework
(RQ-2026-013): how the Picasso–Braque collaboration functioned as an art operation, and
how that operation generated a movement. Scope set to the Bateau-Lavoir years c.
1907–1914, visual arts only.

Built the Art Operation Schema v0.1: 13 dimensions in 4 clusters (I. Core: Telos,
Agents, Site; II. Program: Rhythms, Procedures, Experiments, Materials & tools;
III. Metabolism: Outputs & circulation, Economy, Social infrastructure; IV. Memory:
Documentation, Governance & decision points, Identity).

Designed the evidence-audit + positions-map instrument: one audit card per dimension,
each card carrying what sources say (tagged contemporary/retrospective), corroboration,
missing evidence, granularity, positions, and the sub-question the dimension opens.
Worked Dimension 1 (Telos) as the model card; templated Dimensions 2–13.

## Decisions

- **Decision:** Qualitative case-study method, using my own art-operation framework as
  the analytical grid. / **Reason:** The framework is the contribution; the case study
  is where it gets tested.
- **Decision:** Three timelines, not one — (1) events as they happened, (2) the
  records/discourse timeline, (3) the internal laboratory timeline (largely absent).
  / **Reason:** The gap between (1) and (2) is itself an object of study.
- **Decision:** Honesty convention for every claim — *the record shows* / *we can
  reasonably infer* / *we cannot know*. / **Reason:** Gaps and discontinuities are
  part of the object (Foucault, *Archaeology of Knowledge*); extrapolation must be
  marked as such.
- **Decision:** Start from what records actually contain; "knowing what we don't know"
  comes first. / **Reason:** Documented absence is the method — the New Documentation
  shrinks and maps gaps instead of leaving them unconscious.
- **Decision:** Flag centralization-vs-polycentricity for schema v0.2. / **Reason:**
  The operation was distributed, not centralized; centrality is dimension-specific
  (Picasso/Braque hub of Experiments, Kahnweiler hub of Economy, Apollinaire hub
  of Discourse).

## Problems and friction

## Ideas and sketches

  the Cubism situation (no procedural records, no timesheets) doesn't repeat.
