## What I did

Eight days, and the centre of the practice moved to music. A new
work-line was founded — AlgorithmicMusic, constrained generation
under a ratified spec — and with it a second kind of log. A symphony
begun over thirty years ago was resumed, and its lost first movement
recovered from audio. The Algebra of Workspaces was reviewed,
ruled on, and rebuilt through three versions into doctrine. The
Google archives were rescued in fact: twenty years of photographs
merged and copied, twenty years of mail verified to the byte and
split by year. A wall was met and a ruling made about it. The field
line ran one expedition and one instrument failure, and then rested
by design. Then the window widened past its first draft: the mail
route was rethought after a falsified tool; a whole research line
took its name — Operative Historiography — and opened its first
study, Valéry's Cahiers read by coordinates; the Algebra reached
its version 1.0.0; a short-form video series was born; and the next discourse
was decided, on documentation itself. Tonight, the pushes of the
two logs before this one stand confirmed for the record. What did
not move is not here.

## 2026-10-31 → 2026-11-01 — workspace=[lab] × session_type=[discourse, designing] — the Algebra, ruled and rebuilt
ΔW temp = dense, tightening, conclusive

The Algebra of Workspaces — never ratified, never published, a
working draft — went under review, chunk by chunk, and I ruled on
it. The title is plural, as it should have been since June: an
algebra of workspaces, treating one united workspace at a time.
All seven axes stand — Workspace, Session type, Time, Operator,
Substrate/medium, Project/Work, ΔW temp; striking either of the two
questioned axes would hollow the tuple. ALX is not the Algebra:
ALX is my design workflow methodology; the Algebra is an abstract
language for reasoning about workspaces in general, and the two may
connect without being one beast. The publishable text carries
theory only — no continuation sections, no un-folded addenda; the
full draft, cuts and all, is archived as the undecided reserve.
Cut: game theory (reserved for a Collabrium Theory document, none
exists yet), the energetic game, workstreaming. Kept: complexity
and pull. Then the doctrine sharpened through v0.5, v0.6, and
beyond: THE SWITCH CREATES THE DELTAS — the switch is what I
actually experience, and the deltas are its wake. The record, the
workspace itself, is an extended cognitive organ: a physical,
three-dimensional memory with its very own coordinates.
Precedence — ≺ — is named and given its physics, grain to grain,
the delta as the quantum of change. The workspace is space. The
genetic stratum was disposed of in pieces: the delta chain as
genetic trail, folded in; the dossier question parked, undecided,
on a reading that had not yet arrived. Theory-curation governs the
whole: the best parts only, no more Theory than needed — because
theory here must inform the practice, or it is decoration.

## 2026-11-03 — workspace=[lab] × session_type=[creating, designing] — AlgorithmicMusic founded
ΔW temp = glowing, dense, recursive

A new channel, a new private repository, a new practice-line:
AlgorithmicMusic. Python generates MIDI — constrained, seeded,
recoverable — imported into my sound-design software, each voice
its own track. The specification was ratified the first day and
amended twice inside the window, to v1.2.0. Its load-bearing
rulings: one core module, no script importing another, the core
never quietly changing its behaviour; experiments numbered at
creation, never renumbered, gaps honest; a registry row for each.
And the seed ruling, which is this line's epistemology: every
script always records its seed, so every experiment is fully
recoverable — the deliberate opposite of the GIMP ruling of the
previous log, and for a stated reason: a painting must not repeat,
but an experiment that cannot be re-run cannot be studied. Six
experiments in the founding days — a proof vamp; a Dorian
minor-third cycle; comping under a solo I called perfect; an
oscillation form; a quartal-pedal field with its law of return;
an implied centre with cadence architecture. The harmonic signature
is mine and piano-centred: modulations by thirds, melodies following
the modulated tonality. An analyzer was built to inspect what the
generator makes — generation joined to inspection and reflection,
the survey beside the walk. The standing law over all of it: the
music must be absolutely experimental — plain diatonic correctness
does not count, however correct — randomness belongs in the melody,
and chords need structure. My reflection at the window's middle:
my edge is the simplification of applied theory — small modular
mechanisms, built piece by piece, each step forward only on the
previous experiments' results.

became the working shuttle: the mail's yearly set and the Photos

be reported — file count is the honest progress measure there; a
warm drive and a slowing copy are the medium, not a failure.

ΔW temp = dense, lucid, recursive

A research line took its name. The channel for it was renamed in
place — Operative Historiography: my General Theory of Art
Operations and Workspace Theory, applied to art history. Its first
study opened the same day, from a study pod at the library:
Valéry's Cahiers, read with my Art Operations take — his late-1890s
"système opératoire" subsumed under the General Theory. The
premises are the theory's own: the Cahiers are a workspace in
itself; the morning notebook is the operation; thinking is an
operation, and is an art operation, especially when an artist is
doing the thinking. The next day, at the library, I hand-transcribed
passages from the two thematic volumes; that evening the register
stood at eleven passages, V-0001 to V-0011, dated 1900–1903 — and
my corrected set, delivered that night for the record, is the
canonical text. The method is coordinates: the parenthetical
references are addresses in the twenty-nine-volume facsimile, the
exact location in the physical notebooks, and both other editions
point back to that same address system. From thematic volumes,
chronological edition, and secondary literature I assemble points
into a constellation — the full corpus, some 28,000 pages, is not
accessible to me, and that inaccessibility is itself the
documentation problem the study is built on. The scope is declared:
three technical sections — Système, Mathématique, Science — chosen
because the loan is time-limited, the bias named where it must be.
A negative finding stands in the record: the Pléiade's analytical
index has no entry for "opération" — the operation cluster hides
under other words. A situating axis was added the same evening:
for each claim, was Valéry prescient, repeating what others had
said, or repeating from memory? And tomes III and IX of the
chronological edition were bought, the slow purchase of the set
begun. "Operative" comes from Valéry, including his discourse to
the surgeons: my work is a surgical operation on history — find
where the operation started, and who started it. A whodunit.

## Decisions

**Decision:** In AlgorithmicMusic, every script always records its seed; every experiment is fully recoverable.
**Reason:** An experiment that cannot be re-run cannot be studied. The GIMP ruling stands untouched beside it — code that makes paintings ships non-reproducible — because the two lines have opposite epistemologies, and each ruling names its own.

**Decision:** One core module for the music line; no script imports another; the core never quietly changes its behaviour.
**Reason:** Modular, piece by piece, each step forward only on the previous results — a core that shifts silently would falsify every experiment built on it.

**Decision:** Smart App Control stays on.
**Reason:** Turning it off is irreversible without a full Windows reset. A verified-but-unsigned installer waits, or the work finds another route; the gate is not dismantled for one program.

**Decision:** The second Photos copy goes on a different drive, and no Google deletions happen until it is verified.
**Reason:** The first drive must never be the only home of anything irreplaceable — the rider that has governed every rescue in this practice.

**Decision:** The publishable Algebra carries theory text only; the full draft, with its cuts and continuations, is archived intact as the undecided reserve.
**Reason:** A document ending in un-folded addenda reads as uncertain. Reserved material is kept, and will be reminded of, without standing in the published text.

**Decision:** The mail-reading route through the desktop mail client is abandoned. The replacement route is test-before-faith on the tower — proven on the smallest yearly file first, free, with full-text search of message bodies.
**Reason:** Its routes were falsified against the actual machine, and the mission asked for things my rulings forbid: another full copy, renamed files, configuration surgery trusted blindly. The verified masters are worth more than any reader's convenience.

**Decision:** Moving Noisefields publishing: one 30-second master serves every platform, posted natively on each, never auto-cross-posted; at most one piece published a day, the rest kept as stock. In text fields, No. 001 — never #001.
**Reason:** One master keeps the work identical everywhere; native posts keep each platform's engagement on its own post; and the hash form in a title is a link away from the work.

**Decision:** The Algebra of Workspaces stands at v1.0.0, staged in new-documentation for publication beside the Axioms.
**Reason:** The working-draft years are over. The theory is ready to stand in public exactly as it stands; the drafts freeze as the drafts of record, and the history lives in the logs.
