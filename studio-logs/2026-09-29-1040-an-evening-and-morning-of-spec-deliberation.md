### On spec v1.6 (pending — not yet minted)

Even unfinalized, the design is honest work, and it goes well beyond the earlier specs:

- **Derived, not ad hoc.** The session_type taxonomy is derived from the Historiotheque Work Schema's Facet A instead of accumulated by habit. Every value earns its place against the practice.
- **Two clean axes.** session_type is the verb, workspace the place — orthogonal, both list-valued. The old specs mixed them (field as a session type; lab and studio doing double duty).
- **Variable geometry.** The spec finally models what the practice actually is: a workspace whose nature changes with the work, captured by the *combination* of the axes — a faceted tag language with a century of precedent behind it.
- **Legible bodies.** Boundary markers become active-space state headers: timestamp, space name, formal tags. The log reads as a score, not a string of words.
- **Field honored.** The field artist's half of the practice gets first-class treatment — field as workspace, fieldwork as verbs × field, field logs as a decided need.
- **General inheritance.** v1.6 will sit under a general log-spec that studio, novel, design-concept, and field logs inherit from — new kinds only when a body of records demands them.
