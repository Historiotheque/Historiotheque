# Video discourses: the Treasure and the Open Source Artist

## Session
Two self-recorded video presentations, recorded outdoors in Mont-Saint-Hilaire
on the afternoon of 2026-09-23. The presentations carry overlapping material
drawn from the current research.

## Content
Both videos develop material from the Picasso–Braque collaboration research
(RQ-2026-013, RQ-2026-014):

- The collaboration is extensively documented in its outputs, but the
  day-to-day *procedures* of the practice were not recorded: no account of
  workflow management, workspace management — the ordinary mechanics of two
  people making work side by side.
- The "Treasure" analogy: one would not entrust something of value — one's
  house keys — to a stranger who sold one a bag of apples, "because he seemed
  like a good guy." The same holds for archives. Publishing work on Instagram
  is not archiving; an archive belongs with a trustworthy institution — e.g.
  the Internet Archive — which can be entrusted with the Treasure, whatever
  it may be.
- Art Operations: the first presentation describes applying the best
  practices of software development — systematic logging, documenting,
  archiving — to the running of an Art Operation. Coinage introduced: the
  "Open Source Artist."

## Publication
The first presentation was published the same day to Instagram as a Reel and
as a Story (Instagram-only; no cross-post to Facebook). The second
presentation was recorded in the park by the municipal library; publication
status at time of writing unconfirmed.

Same afternoon, municipal library: borrowed a book on proper documentation

the Jesuit Relations and journals. A planned reading and Wi-Fi working
session was cut short by the heat.
