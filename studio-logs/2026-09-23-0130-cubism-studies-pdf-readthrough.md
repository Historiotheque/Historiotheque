## What I did

Read the Cubism Studies PDF end to end (164 pages, my own working notes from
2002–2003, via text extraction — ~7,300 lines). Mapped its reverse-chronological
structure across 8 sections: Feb 2003 historiography notes (largest section);
March 2003 "Between Bordel and Demoiselles"; Nov 2002 conference drafts; Nov 2002
short notes; Dec 2002 "Another."/anaesthetic notes; Jan 2003 reading/dating notes and
the An/Aesthetic Education program; Jan 2003 Lomazzo/Reformation notes; Jan–Feb 2003
proposals and fragments; closing with the Aug 2002 "New Clockwork" ontology.

Cataloged gleanable material per instrument — audit cards (Dimensions 2, 4, 5–6, 9),
positions map (my stated 2003 position; Leighten/Nochlin as exemplars;
primitivism-language ethics), timelines (events spine: Kodak 1888 through BLAST
1914–15; records spine: Barr 1936 through the 2000s expansion), and bibliography
additions (Gray 1953, Roskill 1985, Fry 1966, Cheetham, Lomas, Leighten, Antliff,
Vallentin 1963, and others). Gleaning into the repo files deferred — token budget.

## Decisions

- **Decision:** Treat the PDF as my own private source material — contributed to the
  discourse privately, not part of the public Discourse on Cubism until a DOI'd paper
  exists. / **Reason:** Keeps the provenance honest: it informs the audit, it doesn't
  count as scholarship yet.
- **Decision:** Flag my Demoiselles dating notes (exhibited 1916/1918, bought 1924,
  New York 1939) as UNVERIFIED — I had marked them "check date" myself in 2003.
  / **Reason:** The honesty convention applies to my own archive first.
- **Decision:** Record the document's Braque-thinness as audit evidence, not just a
  gap. / **Reason:** The record's asymmetry is itself a finding about the operation's
  documentability.
