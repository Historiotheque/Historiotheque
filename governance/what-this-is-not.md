# What This Is Not

(After Foucault's gesture in *The Archaeology of Knowledge* — the attribution is noted here as unverified until checked against a copy. The move is: say what a thing is by saying what it isn't.)

This governance folder is not therapy. It will not heal you, and it does not try to.

It is not financial or business advice. Nothing here is a promise of success — the operation can be an abject failure, and that is within spec. The first rule of the art operation: this might fail drastically. Catastrophic failure. It might ruin your family, your reputation. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something.

It is not an accreditation of experience. Thirty years of practice documented in the light is still one person's practice, not a credential.

It is not a verdict on who is or isn't an artist. This folder governs one operation; it judges no one else's.

What the folder means by a *mature* operation: efficient, continuous, documented, honest. Not lucrative. Maturity was never about money.
