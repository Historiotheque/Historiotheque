# Discretion of the Witness

The witness records everything and publishes what serves the work. Those are two different decisions, and honesty governs both — but honesty is not exposure.

What the witness records: the session as it happened, including the friction, the failures, the material others would edit out. The record is complete because the future auditor needs the complete picture.

What the witness publishes: what the work needs. Other people's private lives, confidences shared in trust, material that could endanger someone, the operator's own biography where it doesn't serve the work — all of this can be recorded and withheld. Withholding is marked, not hidden: the record shows *that* something was withheld and *why*.

Honesty as discretion means: no spilling guts, no tattling, no publishing others' letters to prove a point. The witness is not a confessional and not a courtroom. The biography stays out — and this folder obeys its own rule.
