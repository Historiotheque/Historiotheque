# Recourse and Removal

**If it goes wrong, come to me.**

If someone is harmed by trying a published method, or recognizes themselves in the work — in a character, in an example, in a record — they can contact the operator through his website (URL: [alexgagnon.com](https://alexgagnon.com/)). The operator responds promptly.

On request, an entire document can be removed from the web. No argument, no negotiation about whether the harm was intended. If you recognize yourself and want it down, it comes down.

Warnings without recourse are threats. This page is what makes the warnings ethical: the caution says *be careful*; this page says *and if care wasn't enough, here's the door*.
