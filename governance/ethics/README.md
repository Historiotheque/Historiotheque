# Ethics — Why an Art Operation Has an Ethics Folder

Because methods act on the world. A documented procedure is an instruction; instructions get followed, by people the operator will never meet, in conditions the operator cannot foresee. Publishing methods is publishing causes. Causes need an ethics.

Because the operator is the instrument. The Art Operator is the apparatus the work runs on — and apparatuses have failure modes. The warnings in this folder are maintenance instructions for the instrument.

Because warnings without an ethics are threats. "Don't do this" with no account of why, no interpretation, and no recourse is just intimidation. The ethics folder is what makes the warnings honest: what they mean, what they don't mean, and what happens if something goes wrong anyway.

This folder obeys its own ethics: the biography stays out. What the witness withholds from the record, he withholds from this folder too.
