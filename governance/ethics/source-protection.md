# Source Protection

The journalism analogy holds: people speak to the operation the way sources speak to reporters — sometimes on the record, sometimes not. The default is anonymization. No one becomes a named example without their informed consent.

**Private correspondence is never published.** Not in excerpts, not in paraphrase, not posthumously. Letters, messages, emails from private individuals stay private — full stop. (The operator's own family correspondence is the canonical case: it will never be published, and that decision is not revisited.)

What the record needs from a private source is the *information*, not the identity. Anonymize the person, keep the fact, mark the anonymization. The audit trail shows that something was withheld and why — that is accountability, not a gap in it.
