# Do No Harm

Nothing is published here that could foreseeably endanger anyone — not the operator, not collaborators, not subjects, not strangers.

**Dual use.** Methods are dual-use by nature: a procedure for seeing can become a procedure for surveilling; a technique for persuasion can become a technique for manipulation. The operation publishes methods anyway — that is the whole point of working in the light — but it does not publish the genuinely dangerous ones as procedures. The warnings name the danger; the method stays out. No cases are named here; the principle is stated abstractly on purpose.

Where in doubt, the operation withholds first and reconsiders later. Withholding can be undone. Harm usually can't.
