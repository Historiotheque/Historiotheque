# Governance — The Art Operation @ The Historiotheque

Everything in this folder applies to the operator first.

We're settling accounts.

This folder governs the *operation* of the interdisciplinary independent artist-researcher: how the work is run, documented, checked, and answered for. It is not a manual for life, not a manual for relationships, and not a manual for business or finance. It covers the work and the record of the work — nothing else.

## What this is

The statements of direction (mission, vision, principles), the statement of limits (what this is not), the licensing position, the price of transparency, the ledger of sources, and the ethics folder. Together they are the standing the operation stands on — rules the operator gave himself, in writing, in the light.

The methods documented here are practices, not magic. Writing them down demystifies the arts: there is no secret handshake, only procedures honestly run and honestly recorded.

## What this isn't (yet)

This folder has holes. It is not the definitive state of the discourse — many have written on these things, and this is specifically about *art operations*, from inside one. Where a question is still open, the folder says so. Where a source hasn't been verified, the ledger says so.

This is demanding reading by design. It was written for others, not just the operator — for the young artist fifty years from now, for the stranger with the right to audit. If you read through all of it, you're a champion.

## Accountability, operationalized

Accountability here is accounting: ledgers. The studio logs are the ledger of what happened; the references ledger is the ledger of what was claimed and on what evidence. The audit-card instrument — one card, one dimension: the state of the evidence; constellations of cards, higher-dimensional and indefinitely extensible — is how the operation checks its own claims. It is used in the research, and it is referenced here because that is what it is: accountability, operationalized.

## Custodianship

These are repositories of knowledge. I am merely a Custodian of Knowledge, as I have been initiated in these arts. This folder is part of the transmission: what was learned, written down so it can be carried further.
