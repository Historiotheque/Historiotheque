# References — Ledger of Sources

A ledger, not a bibliography. Every source the governance folder leans on is listed with its verification status. The convention:

- **VERIFIED** — read, passage citable, page numbers where relevant.
- **CANDIDATE** — not yet verified; to check against a copy before it supports any claim.

No unverified sources, ever. A candidate is not a citation — it's a promissory note, marked as one.
