# Licensing

This is an open question, not a ruling. It is documented here because the question matters and the misfit is real: no existing license quite fits what this operation publishes.

The shape of the position so far:

- **Artworks are closed.** No commercial use, no modification, no forgery-as-reproduction. The work is the operator's; the record describes it but does not give it away.
- **Methods are open.** The hour-to-hour procedures — how the work was actually made — are published. This kills the mystique on purpose: there is no secret handshake, only practices.
- **Genuinely dangerous methods are excepted.** What could foreseeably cause harm if reproduced is not published as a procedure. The warnings name the danger; the method stays out. (See ethics/declaration-warnings.md and ethics/do-no-harm.md.)

**Independent invention.** Methods developed independently and recognized afterward in another field are documented as what they are: independent arrivals, not borrowings. The record shows the dates.

**Why no license is named.** The operation publishes *cultural software* — documented procedures for art-research — and the existing licenses were written for software code, texts, or images, not for this. The misfit is documented, not solved. When it is solved, this page will say so.

**A historical note.** Undocumented procedure was once hoarded intellectual capital: the old model monetized secrecy — the master's methods died with the master, or were sold dear. This operation defects from that economy. The methods are the commons; the artworks are the property. That is the whole of the licensing philosophy until further notice.
