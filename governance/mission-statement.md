# Mission Statement — The Art Operation @ The Historiotheque

The mission of the Art Operation @ The Historiotheque is to conduct interdisciplinary art-research in the open: to make images, sounds, and texts while documenting the making so completely that any honest stranger — a young artist fifty years from now, or the operator himself in ten — can audit the record, reproduce the experiments, and carry the methods further.

The work and the record of the work are one practice, conducted in the light. Nothing hidden, nothing reconstructed after the fact without being labeled as reconstruction. What the operation claims, the record must be able to show.
