# PATTERN-004: The Through-Bound Cost Triad

**Context:** Running the operation as an economic system — streamline it, measure it, pay less.

**Problem:** Costs hide in the middle. Inbound and outbound are visible (messages in, artifacts out); what happens between them — the processing — is where attention quietly burns.

**Solution:** Account costs at three loci, each with its own instrument:

- **Inbound** — the cost of reception: detecting, interrupting, classifying, deciding. Instrument: the Signal Types taxonomy (PATTERN-002). Bill ambiguity to the sender.
- **Through-bound** — the cost of processing within the operation: walking procedures, producing λs, deciding which steps apply. Throughput as its own concept, distinct from inputs and outputs. Instrument: decision trees (PATTERN-001). A procedure with no tree is an unmeasured cost.
- **Outbound** — the cost of transmission: composing, classifying, sending — plus the receiver's attention on the far end. Instrument: well-formed signals (PATTERN-002). Every emission priced before it leaves.

Review the triad on a rhythm: where did the week burn? Shrink the hottest locus first.

**Worked example:** Cutting a release — inbound: triage the week's feedback, classifying each item; through-bound: walk the release tree (build, version, document, publish — no improvisation); outbound: one announcement signal, fully classified, instead of five partial ones.
