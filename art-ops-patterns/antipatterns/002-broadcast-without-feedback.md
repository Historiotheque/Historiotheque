# ANTIPATTERN-002: Broadcast Without Feedback

**Context:** Any outbound channel — social feeds, release announcements, newsletters.

**Symptoms:** The operation emits steadily and reads nothing back. Comments go unanswered, or unnoticed. The audience is treated as a void to fill rather than a Stranger to encounter. Over time the signals get louder and emptier: pure carrier, zero payload.

**The failure:** Forgetting what the system is for. The receiver is the most important gear in the machine — the Encounter with the Stranger is the reason the artifacts ship at all. A broadcast channel with the feedback path severed is a monologue pretending to be a conversation.

**Remedy:** PATTERN-002 for the emissions; plus a standing intake for the channel itself — read the replies, classify them with the same taxonomy, and let them change the next emission. The ambient space talks back; the operation listens.

**Related:** PATTERN-002, PATTERN-004 (outbound costs include the cost of not listening).
