# ANTIPATTERN-001: The Staging-Area Tar Pit

**Context:** Any backlog, inbox, or "to file" folder.

**Symptoms:** The pile grows monotonically. Items enter dated and leave never. The operator avoids opening it. Duplicate systems appear — the archive and the pile. Energy goes to managing guilt about the pile instead of emptying it.

**The failure:** Intake mistaken for progress. Collecting 100 design concepts feels like 100 novels begun; it is 100 novels not begun. The designing phase is cheap and thrilling; the implementation phase is where books get written. A staging area with no outflow is a machine for converting momentum into guilt.

**Remedy:** PATTERN-003 — run the pipeline to the end, on a rhythm, and let version control be the Done column. Shrink the pile; don't reorganize it.
