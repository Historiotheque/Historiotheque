# Annotated Taxonomy of Art & Research Concepts (Post–August 7, 2026)

Intro (from the source document): The documents created or uploaded since August 7, 2026, focus on key areas of the art-research practice, including applied archival therapy, systemic art operations, the scientific lineages of surrealist automatism, and speculative literary architecture.

## 1. Applied Phenomenology & Archival Therapy (Archive Art)
- Historiotherapeusis & Temporal Reactivation
    - Extraction from Inertia: Isolating individual physical or digital nodes (audio fragments, margin notes) out of chaotic decay and structuring them into intentional compositions.
    - Phenomenological Re-activation: Presenting curated arrangements back to a subject to provoke active re-inhabitation and re-authoring of their personal timeline, rather than passive recollection.
    - Living Inscription / In-Situ Field Recording: Capturing real-time vocal hesitations, ambient room sounds, and sensory reactions as the subject interacts with the archive, creating a self-ingesting feedback loop.
- Critique of "Automated Ghosting"
    - Authenticated Transmission vs. Synthetic Simulation: Contrasts high-touch, conscious curation during life with post-mortem AI voice simulation ("ghost tech"), prioritizing existential integration for the creator over synthetic grief mitigation for survivors.
- Multimodal Intervention Formats
    - Historiophanic Chambers: Spatialized acoustic and tactile environments where physical objects (e.g., bone-conduction transducers in vintage furniture) trigger audio-spatial nodes to induce a sudden collapse of historical distance (historiophany).
    - Counter-Archival Mapping of Historiopathies: Highlighting structural silences, abrupt lineage ruptures, and systemic historical traumas rather than filling gaps with comforting fictions.

## 3. Scientific Lineages of Automatism & Involuntary Drawing
- 19th-Century Scientific Roots
    - The Graphic Method & Indexical Trace: Tracing Surrealist psychic automatism back to physiological recording devices (Marey's myographs, kymographs) that converted invisible physical forces into graphical traces.
    - Conferring Objectivity: Utilizing the visual authority of scientific recordings (white curvilinear traces on dark fields) to legitimate unconscious mental phenomena.
- Empirical Writing Protocols (Les Champs Magnétiques)
    - Experimental Parameters: Strict elimination of critical filters through maximum writing speed, absolute prohibition of post-facto editing, and deliberate physiological exhaustion.
    - Dual-Subject Setup: Concurrent side-by-side writing sessions (Breton & Soupault) to test for inter-subjective symbolic resonance and cross-validate automatic mechanisms.
- Post-War & Contemporary Re-Inscriptions
    - Shift from Expression to Entropy: Reinterpreting the automatic trace away from Freudian psychic determinism toward physical mechanics, cosmic background radiation (Jem Finer), linguistic dissolution (Peter Rose), and somatic resistance (William Anastasi, Rebecca Horn).

## 4. Phenomenological Novelistics & Archival Architecture
- The "Archives-Land" Thought Experiment
    - Global Storage Polytope: Modeling planet Earth as a massive warehouse complex for physical and digital data storage, drawing parallels to Borges' Library of Babel.
- Mechanism Design & Autonomous Systems
    - Task Distribution Networks: Structuring narratives through distributed task assignments across real and simulated human/machine agents (DAOs).
    - The Powerless Voyeur God: Narrator position that observes the inner workings of the author's mind without the ability to intervene in the system.
- Formal Theories of Creativity
    - Predictor-Compressor Dynamics: Integrating Jürgen Schmidhuber's formal theory of creativity, where intrinsic reward stems from measuring the learning progress and subjective data compression of regular patterns.
