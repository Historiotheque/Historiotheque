# ALX-Extensible Faceted Schema (v.1.0) + Global Research Schema (GRS)

## The ALX-Extensible Faceted Schema (v.1.0)

### Facet 1: [M] Methodological Framework (The Engine)
- [M-ALX] - ALX Methodology (Current implementation)
- [M-ALX2] - ALX Methodology (Future branch/iteration)
- [M-HIS] - Historiomics / Database Arts methodologies
- [M-FLD] - General Field Art methodologies
- [M-SYS] - Meta-systemic (Notes about the methodologies themselves, architectural blueprints)

### Facet 2: [V] Architectural Vector (The Domain)
- [V-ACQ] Acquisition Vector: The Field. Gathering, observing, pulling external signals inward.
- [V-EXE] Execution Vector: The Lab. High-intensity production, tactical generation, parameter testing.
- [V-STB] Stabilization Vector: The Forge. Editing, refining, packaging, and preparing for external reality.
- [V-FDB] Feedback Vector: The Network. Discourse, monitoring reception, dissemination.
- [V-MEM] Memory Vector: The Archive. Historiographical deep-storage, post-hoc documentation, retrieval.

### Facet 3: [N] Operational Node (The Specific Phase)
- [N-SCN] Scanning (Ambient data gathering)
- [N-RSH] Research (Polytextual synthesis, active reading)
- [N-CON] Concept (Defining the thesis/intent)
- [N-MTH] Methodology Design (Building the constraints/toolchains)
- [N-PRM] Parameters (Setting boundary variables)
- [N-EXP] Experimentation (Active generation/generation)
- [N-DOC-I] Documentation (In-Vivo / raw logging)
- [N-REF] Refinement (Polishing, editing)
- [N-SHP] Shipping (Packaging, publishing)
- [N-DIS] Discourse (Reception, critique)
- [N-DSM] Dissemination (Secondary syndication/routing)
- [N-REV] Review (Retrospective systemic analysis)
- [N-DOC-P] Documentation (Post-Hoc / formalized rules)
- [N-ARC] Archiving (Cold storage, database writing)
- [N-RTV] Retrieval (Pulling from the archive)

### Facet 4: [T] Temporal & Attentional State (The Rhythm)
- [T-ORG] Organic Time: Low-frequency, unstructured, drifting cognition (walking, passive listening).
- [T-CLK] Clock Time: High-frequency, 15-to-20-minute micro-tasking, high physical/cognitive tension.
- [T-ARC] Deep Time: Post-archival reflection, engaging with decadal loops, historical mapping.

### Facet 5: [O] Trajectory & Orientation (The Momentum)
- [O-FWD] Forward Propulsion: Moving strictly to the next sequential node (e.g., Concept -> Methodology).
- [O-REC] Recursive Iteration: Locked in a micro-loop (e.g., cycling rapidly between Experiment <-> Refine).
- [O-LAT] Lateral Leap: An emergent rerouting or paradigm shift (e.g., an anomaly in the Lab throws you instantly back to Field Scanning).
- [O-RTL] Retrospective Lineage: Looking backward into the history of the system to inform a current block.

### Facet 6: [S] Substrate (The Material Reality)
- [S-REF] Refcard (Physical index card, bridging stations)
- [S-RAW] Raw Field Data (Audio logs, sketches, unparsed text)
- [S-DIG] Digital Lab Asset (Code, raw canvas file, DAW project)
- [S-FIN] Immutable Master (The shipped, finished artifact)

## Global Research Schema (GRS)

### Facet A: Theoretical & Philosophical Frameworks (THEO)
The primary intellectual lenses used to decode cultural and historical phenomena.
- THEO.1 — Critical Theory & Constellation Historiography
- THEO.2 — Phenomenology, Affect & Sensation
- THEO.3 — Cybernetics & Systems Theory
- THEO.4 — Historiomics & Historiopathies

### Facet B: Art History & Aesthetic Movements (HIST)
The specific spatial, formal, and historical sites under examination.
- HIST.1 — Modernism & Avant-Garde Collaborations
- HIST.2 — Cubism & Spatial Reconfiguration
- HIST.3 — The Aesthetics of Weathering & Patina
- HIST.4 — Database Arts & Non-Linear Narratives

### Facet C: Formal Mechanics & Operational Logic (MECH)
The functional structure of the concept or the artifact being analyzed.
- MECH.1 — Polyphony & Counterpoint
- MECH.2 — Montage, Collage & Cut-up
- MECH.3 — Translation, Transcription & Remediation
- MECH.4 — Compression & Micro-Structure

### Facet D: Epistemic Status (EPI)
The current condition of this card within your cognitive architecture.
- EPI.1 — Primary Fragment
- EPI.2 — Connective Tissue
- EPI.3 — Speculative Specimen
- EPI.4 — Archival Pruning

### Structural Combinations in Action (examples)
- The Picasso-Braque Passages — THEO.2 / HIST.2 / MECH.2 / EPI.1 — A primary observation on how Cubist collage acts as a phenomenological shift in spatial representation.
- Benjamin's Method of Composition — THEO.1 / HIST.4 / MECH.2 / EPI.2 — A connective synthesis linking Benjamin's fragmentary montage technique directly to non-linear database structures.
- Erosion as Historical Coding — THEO.4 / HIST.3 / MECH.3 / EPI.3 — A highly speculative card exploring how the physical patina on an object can be read as a structural historical error (historiopathy).
