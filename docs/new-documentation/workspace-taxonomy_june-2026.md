# Historiotheque Workspace Taxonomy — June 2026

*Annotated taxonomy of subjects in the June 2026 log notes (A.G.). This is a
**finding aid** — an index to the log, not a concept. For the concepts themselves, see the [DesignConcepts](https://github.com/Historiotheque/DesignConcepts) repository and the [Refcards](https://github.com/Historiotheque/Refcards) repository.

The source is a dense, timestamped personal research-and-practice log spanning
early-to-mid June 2026. It functions simultaneously as daily activity record,
theoretical workshop, creative production journal, self-audit, and speculative
manifesto — the voice of an independent interdisciplinary artist-researcher running
an autonomous "art operation" under the name **Historiotheque**.

## 1. Core institutional / operational framework: Historiotheque & Art Operations

- **Historiotheque**: the mobile art studio / sound-design + research laboratory —
  a "mobile witness-box," portable cultural lab, variable-geometry space reconfigurable
  between art studio, recording studio, interior-design laboratory, or field unit.
  Ambient space treated as the primary medium.
- **Art Operations / Art Operators**: practitioners as "art operators" running
  autonomous "art operations." Framed as Strategic Intermedia Arts, Info-Op Art,
  Tactical Media, Database Arts — with a martial undertone: the operator as cognitive
  architect on "Planet Historiosphere."
- **Seasons of the Heart**: seasonal, liturgical rhythm of production — high-velocity
  creative seasons followed by deliberate shutdowns/hiatuses. Linked to the year-long
  secular liturgical song cycle of the same name.
- **Design Web Labs (DesignWebLabs)**: an additional conceptual layer.
- **Antilog series**: timestamped public writing (e.g. ANTILOG_01June26a) as formalized
  research updates and self-reviews.

## 2. Workspace Theory / Delta-Workspace / Algebra of the Workspace

The most elaborated original theoretical cluster in the log.

- **Continuously changing surface / geology of the workspace**: the workspace as a
  living, deformable 3D/4D surface whose patina and evolution are the primary object
  of documentation and research.
- **Elastic / Fluid / Adaptive Interface Envelope**: dynamic screen real-estate and
  horizon planes; continuous deformation rather than rigid windows.
- **Algebra of the Workspace**: explicit mathematical analogy — "solving for X," where
  the unknown is hypothesized as the strange attractor (or attractor basin) of the
  trajectory of the surface-functions over time and space.
- **Delta-Workspace (ΔW) & Historiotopic Velocity (v_h)**: progress measured by
  qualitative/structural deltas between states rather than completed goals; velocity of
  change (gentle deformation vs. rupture) as a key operational metric.
- **Offloading cognitive load onto the surface**: externalizing memory, status, and
  history into the physical/digital environment to maintain Distributed Situational
  Awareness without collapse.
- **Hyperreflexive Workspace Model of Intelligence**: the laboratory itself as the
  medium of intelligence (human, artificial, or hybrid) — intelligence as ongoing
  orchestration and mutation of a pliable field.

## 3. Workstreaming / Worldstream / Interstreaming

A personal concept developed independently, then discovered to have prior art.

- **Workstreaming**: real-time or near-real-time documentation, broadcasting, and
  sharing of autonomous artistic/research operations as a live evolving stream —
  parallel to (but narrower and elevated from) Gelernter's lifestreaming; focused
  exclusively on intentional cultural labor.
- **Global Workstream / Worldstream**: the interstreaming of N autonomous individual
  workstreams into a higher-order stream-of-streams; ambient rooms "superimpose" as
  mixed signals while retaining unique room dynamics.
- **Prior art acknowledged**: 1962 manufacturing usage (Charles Rumford Walker),
  project-management jargon (PMBOK, PRINCE2), Anne Zelenka's 2007 "workstreaming,"
  software libraries, standards bodies, commercial products.
- **Military parallels researched**: Common Operational Picture (COP), 4D-COP,
  Network-Centric Warfare self-synchronization, Link 16, spatial audio in cockpits,
  Power to the Edge doctrine.

## 4. Historical Topology (HistTop / Historiomic Topology) & related ontologies

The most formalized theoretical system in the later entries.

- **Axioms of Historiomic Topology**: (1) Relational Proximity — resonance/influence
  over chronology/geography; (2) Topological Invariance and Deformation — continuity
  as gentle deformation, rupture as tear; (3) Structural Equivalence / Homeomorphism —
  "history rhymes"; (4) Topological Singularity / Knots — dense, irresolvable
  entanglements such as colonialism.
- **Phenomenological correlates**: Sillage (atmospheric wake of past events),
  historiophanic shock/trauma, archetypal sillage, entanglement/difficulty.
- **Patho-Topology**: Historiotomes (high-curvature zones); Historiopathies
  (tearing/trauma scars, voids/erasure, ossification/sclerosis, pathological
  knots/compulsion loops).
- **Historionautics**: active traversal of history as participant rather than
  spectator; co-curation of emergent historical environments.
- **Chronotronics & Historiotronics**: time as computable topology — temporal loop
  protocols, ontological entanglement models, chrono-sillage decay engines;
  historiophanic signatures and playable counterfactual ontologies.
- Proposed applications: AI world models, multi-agent coordination via a shared
  Unified Field of Experience / Historiotopia, geopolitics, collaborative interfaces.

## 5. Creative production streams

- **Music & sound design** (dominant practical focus by mid-June): organ compositions
  ("HARLEQUIN," "PETIT LAPIN DANS L'HERBE"); discourses ("MUSIC OF THE OLD VILLAGE,"
  "Morning/Evening Constitutional" series); technical research (flanger, phaser,
  presence range, Dolby Atmos, spatial audio); field recordings and atmospheric tones;
  the year-long "Seasons of the Heart" song cycle and "Symphony of the Old Village"
  (acoustic landscape of St-Hilaire).
- **Visual art**: acrylic painting "THE EYE OF THE MINISTRY"; digital aura (contra
  Benjamin); Refcard systems in 3D space; mode-to-mode translation of effects
  (sound ↔ visual).
- **Video practice**: frequent YouTube uploads of talks, compositions, and field work
  under #historiotheque #artoperations; preference for "ad lib" style after
  information saturation.
- **Writing**: Antilog entries, theoretical essays, occasional poetry, novelistic
  fragments (Chronotopium / The Archivillus Fragments), metafictional concerns
  (author on trial, failure as form).

## 6. Military / defense analogies and potential applications

Extensive mapping of personal concepts onto military doctrine and technology:
Cubism → Section de Camouflage precedent; workspace surface → 4D-COP / Synthetic
Common Operating Environment; spatial audio for cognitive separation of streams;
Red Teaming, Science Fiction Prototyping, Narrative Warfare (DARPA, NATO Innovation
Hub, Army Cyber Command); organic-time resynchronization vs. rigid clock-time;
the artist-researcher positioned as Cognitive Architect.

## 7. Theological / mystical / philosophical substrate

- Dedication of all work to "that One Thing" that keeps the heart pure (Catholic
  mystical and ascetic theology — "to will one thing").
- Desert Fathers (Evagrius, St Anthony), St Mary of Egypt, psalmody as participation
  in the music of the spheres, virtues/vices, the restoration of all things.
- Foucault's epistemic ruptures and archaeologies of knowledge.
- Buckminster Fuller: "build a new model that makes the existing model obsolete."
- Walter Benjamin on aura (critiqued and updated for digital media).
- Broader references: Deleuze, Derrida, Kierkegaard, Virginia Woolf, Dziga Vertov.

## 8. AI, world models & spatial intelligence

- Critique of AI researchers' impoverished experiential models of workspaces.
- Proposal: Delta-Workspace Theory, Historical Topology, and hyperreflexive laboratory
  practice as essential prior art for next-generation world models and spatial
  intelligence.
- Nonlinear/montage-like experience (Burroughs cut-up), zero-memory strategies,
  perturbed networks, multi-agent coordination via shared environmental deltas.
- The Schizobot repository as a concrete implementation vehicle.

## 9. Methodological & meta-practice notes

- Timestamped markdown micro-logs, Refcards, printed research metabolized at the desk.
- Workflow formalization as a distinguishing feature of the practice.
- Recurring self-audits: independent rediscovery of prior art; the risk of appearing
  as a "deceiver"; insistence that the application to art-research practice remains
  original.
- Debates on AI-generated images vs. text, labeling standards, sincerity in online
  discourse.
- Organizational principle: works divided into **Projects** containing multi-genre
  **Series**.

## 10. Peripheral / occasional subjects

Project-management literature (PMBOK 6th, APM BoK 7th, PRINCE2); technical notes on
video resolutions and fonts; specific YouTube video IDs; reflections on sincerity,
social media, the "Circus-Machine" allegory of creative life, interruptions by the
"Invisible Forces of History."

## Overall character

The notes constitute a continuous, self-documenting experimental system in which daily
practice, theoretical invention, historical research, military analogy, theological
devotion, and technological speculation are treated as mutually modulating streams
within a single global workstream. The dominant ambition is the formalization of a
hyperreflexive, historically deep, spatially elastic laboratory practice — serving
simultaneously as personal art operation, contribution to AI research, and resource
for cognitive innovation. The document itself is the primary artifact of that
practice: a living, timestamped surface of the workspace.

**A note on what this is.** This repository belongs to an ongoing research-creation
project at the intersections of art, history, and philosophy — the open working
record of one artist-researcher's practice. It documents a method, not a manual:
nothing here is instruction, counsel, or advice on running an art operation, a
studio, or a creative life, and nothing here is presented as a model to follow.
What holds in this laboratory may not hold in yours.

All works are offered in good faith as contributions to public discourse and
aesthetic reflection. Take what is useful and leave the rest — the responsibility
for interpretation, and for whatever is done with it, remains with each participant
in that dialogue.
