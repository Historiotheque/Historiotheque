# Studio Log Vocabulary

*Companion to the Studio Log Format Specification (filed beside it, in
new-documentation/specs/). Created 2026-10-04.*

This document defines the controlled vocabulary of the `ΔW temp` line — the
QoT (qualities of time) qualia that read a studio log segment's workspace
temperature. Each ΔW temp line carries three qualia, derived from the segment's
record, proposed at drafting, and ratified by the Operator.

**How terms enter:** a term enters this vocabulary only when a real log first
uses it and the Operator ratifies that use. Nothing is pre-invented and nothing
is added ad hoc — the vocabulary grows from the logs, never ahead of them. Each
entry carries the date it was introduced and the context of its first use. Terms
are never removed; a term that falls out of use simply stops being proposed.

**Form:** qualia only. There are no hue terms in this vocabulary — the three
adjectives carry the whole reading (the Operator's ruling, 2026-10-04).

---

**conclusive** — time that closes something: a long arc completing inside the
segment; the quality of an ending that was earned.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · first used: the Soft 5.0.0 restoration segment, 2026-10-03, completing the Official Releases archive (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*

**dense** — many deltas packed into the span; the surface changed in many
places at once, and the time feels thick with work.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · the Operator's own word, from the moraltempcards practice · first used: the Workspace Theory temperature segment, 2026-10-03 (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*

**exact** — careful, precise work: measurements, restorations, verifications;
time spent getting a thing exactly right.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · first used: the 5.0.0 restoration segment, 2026-10-03 (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*

**glowing** — the segment holds a luminous moment: an insight, a graceful error,
an encounter that lit the work and warmed the record around it.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · the Operator's own word, from the moraltempcards practice · first used: the Workspace Theory temperature segment, 2026-10-03 (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*

**joyful** — time coloured by gladness in the work itself; the quality the
season's name knows.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · first used: the field session with a friend, 2026-10-03 (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*

**loose** — unstructured time; the configuration held loosely, play over
procedure, the next move found by feel.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · first used: the field session with a friend, 2026-10-03 (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*

**lucid** — clear-seeing time; distinctions resolve and the shape of a problem
becomes visible whole.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · first used: the derivation segment, Workspace Theory, 2026-10-04 (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*

**methodical** — step-by-step time; a procedure followed in order, nothing
skipped, each step verified before the next.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · first used: the 5.0.0 restoration segment, 2026-10-03 (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*

**polyphonic** — several projects or channels held in parallel inside the
segment, each keeping its own line, sounding together rather than in sequence —
as voices in polyphony.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · first used: the Workspace Theory temperature segment, 2026-10-03 (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*

**raw** — unprocessed time; the work in its first, unrefined state, before any
treatment.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · first used: the field session with a friend, 2026-10-03 (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*

**recursive** — the work loops back on itself: the segment revises, re-derives,
or re-reads its own earlier states, and the return is the work.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · first used: the derivation segment, Workspace Theory, 2026-10-04 (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*

**settling** — time in which things come to rest in their places; the surface
stilling after a long period of change.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · first used: the Soft 5.0.0 restoration segment, 2026-10-03 (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*

**tightening** — the span narrows toward a point: vagueness squeezed out, a
definition or a decision approaching its final form.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · first used: the derivation segment, Workspace Theory, 2026-10-04 (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*

**unhurried** — time with slack in it; no deadline pressure inside the segment;
the work allowed to take the time it takes.
*Introduced 2026-10-04 · first used: the 5.0.0 restoration segment, 2026-10-03 (studio log 2026-10-04-0516).*
