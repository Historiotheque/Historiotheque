# Official Release of The Historiotheque Semantic Version 5.2.0 (MINOR VERSION) — summary

A big MINOR version: everything in it is backward-compatible; nothing has been broken. After the clean break of 5.0.0 (May 2026), which rebuilt the infrastructure from the ground up, this release adds features on top of that new architecture and streamlines the whole operation. The state of the operation: running. The summer went as summers go — a hiatus, then a fallow stretch, production continuing daily at lower intensity — and September brought the relaunch, more productive than ever. A few more paintings were added to The Chronotopium Series, introduced in the last release. The season named: the Autumn of Joyful Being.

The idea of the release: formalize The Historiotheque even more, streamline it, put it into repositories — an organization on GitHub, a community on Zenodo, the whole Verifiability Stack. Concretely:

- **Governance and formalization.** A governance folder — the first constitutional layer of the operation: mission, vision, a statement of principles, ethics documents. The studio log spec minted twice: v1.6 live September 30, superseded by v1.7 on October 4, with a Studio Log Vocabulary of 14 terms. Workspace Theory: Axioms and Consequences, v1.0.0. The Formalization of the Work-System begun, module by module. A doctrine now governs the versions themselves: a version is assigned at publication, not during drafting. The surge-and-collapse cycle now goes by the name Workspace Collapse Protocol (also expressed as the Surge-and-Collapse Protocol).
- **Instruments and archives.** The Official Releases of the past ten years preserved in full text: the complete archive, 19 documents from 2016 to 2026, converted to Markdown with images and videos restored, under a new Official Release spec. The Declarations archive is live on GitHub. The Laboratory Notebook revived (Historiotheque Series #001). Refcards and Research Questions continue in strict sequence.
- **Repos and infrastructure.** The Historiotheque GitHub organization is in daily use — declarations, statements, studio logs, refcards, video discourses, governance. The site historiotheque.github.io is live, historiotheque.ca its custom domain. Commit signing is in place. The local-clone workflow on GitHub Desktop is now the standard way of working — the Cultural DevOps turn, as it has been called in passing.
- **Research.** The Cubism audit continues as a research program; its video is "WHAT IS CUBISM? An Art Operation, 1907-1914" (September 23, 2026). Historiophany of the first kind — the Cézanne realization — opened a new line in September. The Historiomics formalization corpus has been assembled and read in full. Noise Fields continues, in Affinity now — a new painting, "AUTUMN OF ATONEMENT," this month. Paintings in the Historiotheque Series are in progress, studied carefully, layers and possibly collage to be added. The 2025 piece "Assisted by Machine Intelligence" has been rewritten for the agentic era, appearing on the operator's own Medium and Substack accounts alongside this release.
- **Writings.** "The Freeport of St-Hilaire" (short story), "Abstract Characterology" (a book on the character-development method), and the novel "The Chronotopium: A Story of Algorithmogenesis" are in progress; work on the ExperimentalNovel repository as a whole — The Nihilist, Historiophany, and older unfinished novels. A flaw found in the novel-writing enterprise: fully-formed design concepts for whole 1000-page novels arrive in the blink of an eye, but writing them out is the bottleneck — the solution is one page at a time, pages added slowly, rather than 1000 pages at once across a dozen novels in progress.
- **Strategy.** Bluesky is the main hub; YouTube carries everything sonic and the video discourses. A self-directed PhD-equivalent cursus is outlined, its flagship goal the stylometric analysis of the operator's own prose across twenty years.

One piece of bookkeeping closes with this release: the versions of the Art Operation itself. Launched at v5.0.1 when The Historiotheque opened in August 2016 (its previous versions across 13 locations, 20+ years), unofficially at v5.0.2 in the Abject Failure document a month later, stated as 4.0 in March 2023, 5.0 later in 2023, and run at a new MAJOR version 6.0.0 alongside The Historiotheque v4.2.0 in May 2024 — the Art Operation's numbers were never one clean ladder; the count accumulated accounting errors, transcription errors, operator errors. The dual versioning served its purpose when the operation and the place needed separate books; they no longer do. The Art Operation is therefore given the symbolic version 7.0.0 — a fresh start, and its final version. From now on there is one version track only: the Official Releases of The Historiotheque. (Repository releases — ExperimentalNovel v2.0.0, for example — are a separate type of release, not to be confused with these.)

Follow-through: 4.3.1's declaration that no future release would break backward compatibility was broken deliberately by 5.0.0, because it had to be; this release restores the backward-compatible line, on the new architecture. The June 7, 2025 shutdown promise — to properly document everything and make it public — is now kept in full, the complete full-text archive converted and published. The Quick Update's anticipated 4.4.1 never deployed; the MAJOR arrived as 5.0.0. And 5.0.0's promised Important Updates and textual content were continued by 5.1.0 (July 3, 2026) and by this release.

What's next: a review of the last Official Declaration of Production-Year — no Important Update needed, since the Declaration published in May is one year ahead and covers the following year as well; the Deep-Archives Project moving forward — new hard drives with a couple of terabytes of space, everything going onto them, with its own scripts for data analysis and data visualization of the digital archives; a lot of programming, mostly procedural generation and procedural texture, the Refcard system, and text engines; reader summaries for the archive to be decided and written; the Formalization of the Work-System continuing module by module; the Historiomics formalization joined to the scale of the individual art operation; the Cubism audit continuing.

What is not done: the reader summaries are not yet written. The reconstruction note in the archive README is still provisional wording. And a Retrospective — to be done periodically, as the Spring Retrospective was — is planned, but not yet written.
