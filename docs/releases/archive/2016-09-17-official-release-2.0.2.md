The workspace is back online. We start working again on Monday, the 19th of September, 2016. After a little over a day of operations last week, we shut everything down for data analysis. We rebuilt everything from scratch over the course of the following four days. Now we are taking the weekend off to prepare for relaunch. If everything runs as it should, we open shop again on Monday morning.

We maybe lost a week of work, but we are better positioned now than we were before. We are very satisfied with the work that we have done heretofore. May Holy Monday live up to its name and be the bestMonday of the three last quarters, at least. That’s the record we want to beat, at least for us here working at The Historiotheque.

Unfortunately, until we can find and fix any bugs with the current release v.2.0.2 of the Art House, The Historiotheque, we are not taking in any new assignments. We maybe completed all of the work for Production-Year 2017–2018 on Day One of operations for 2016, but the world doesn’t stop there. We are working on the official Declaration for Autumn 2017, for the Production-Year 2018–2019, amongst a few other things, until further notice. Pax vobiscum.

Sincerely,

A.G.
