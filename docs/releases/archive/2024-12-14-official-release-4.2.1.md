I

I have had a pretty intense fallow period in my Art Operation since about June, 2024. We are now in the middle of December, 2024, soon to enter into anno domini, 2025. However, the Historiotheque is up and running again in full force, greater than ever as usual, and it now has a brand new semantic version 4.2.1., which is technically just a patch, which is reserved for when I make backward compatible bug fixes.

The bug fix in question had to do with a distinct passivity in my slow period of relative inaction. I still worked on all sorts of projects, did a lot of badly needed reading and kept writing every day at a good enough pace. My last big period of intense activity, creativity, and productivity goes back to last May, 2024. This is a preliminary launch for what seems like is going to be a winter of deep work and of great production at the highest standard.

No real, extra functionality was added in this semantic version, only as patch as stated above. The patch was fixing a bug as I was saying in the previous more passive phase and I discovered an anomaly in the process of trying to go towards greater and greater mobilization, slowly building up to this culmination-point:

***A Summer of The Heart Acting as A Winter of The Soul!***

What does this mean? It means that there are *many types of seasons.*

There are the four main seasons of course, but countless other seasons, of all types, of all shapes and forms. Each season has its own unique liturgical expressions across three disciplines or "modes": Image(s), Sound(s), and Text(s). This forms the secular liturgy of The Seasons of The Heart song-cycle as well as the analog and digital paintings and fiction and non-fiction writings that go with it.

This summer, then, was a kind of "cold summer". It was indeed a long almost 7-month long fallow period with very little activity at times. (The Art Operation, which also has a semantic version, is not officially at v.6.0.1.)

II

I wrote the following in my Official Release of May 23rd, 2024:

> In any case, I am not necessarily "officially shutting down The Art Operation", but I am however officially "on hiatus", until further notice. I will still be producing art and conducting research. That never stops. But I will spend the rest of the summer and then the spring mostly walking, reading, meditating, writing, and spending time also with those who are closest to my heart in my personal life. I'm also presently reworking my entire MEDIA STRATEGY, the "strategic" aspect of my business operation. — (SEE: [The Latest Official Release of The Historiotheque Semantic Version 4.2.0.](https://medium.com/historiotheque/the-latest-official-release-of-the-historiotheque-semantic-version-4-2-0-38c9ba1c98bb))

I did exactly what I said I would do, as always. That's part of the process I went through over the last decade at least of making the Art Operation *dependable.*

And if you have been following these declarations for a while, you will know all too well that I always say that the Art Operation goes up and just as quickly comes back down. It gets BUILT UP, then DRAWN DOWN. Therefore, a fallow period was expected, and I said as much.

The fallow period has a bug in it, though, a "noise-bit" as I like to call them, which created "logopathic" processes which echoed throughout the period and took many different forms, creating a kind of "personal historopathy".

Finally, as I said back on that fateful day of May 23rd, I will continue working on the [Creative Work-System called ALX](https://medium.com/historiotheque/the-creative-work-system-called-alx-1-0-0-53a5ebbcb4d5), which is a *"rigorous design workflow management methodology"*. Other projects include, the systematic documentation of [Database Arts](https://medium.com/historiotheque/database-arts-570baccb3f02), [The Refcards-Project](https://medium.com/historiotheque/the-refcards-project-35c680240cc9), [The New Documentation](https://medium.com/historiotheque/the-new-documentation-1e3f863719b6), [The AntiOS Concept](https://medium.com/historiotheque/the-antios-concept-e2e482d0eba3), [The Project-Based Serial Method](https://medium.com/historiotheque/the-project-based-serial-method-9dfd0bebd0e9), [The Switchboard Method of Project-Based, Serial Art Production](https://medium.com/historiotheque/the-switchboard-method-of-project-based-serial-art-production-9e8fc607a94a), [The Art-Ops-In-A-Box Concept](https://medium.com/historiotheque/the-art-ops-in-a-box-concept-41e61979f2b3), one on [Noise in The Workspace](https://medium.com/historiotheque/noise-in-the-workspace-764c96a0ad4e), another on [Foundation Models](https://medium.com/historiotheque/foundation-models-83be35096b96), one on [Interdisciplinary Art-Research](https://medium.com/historiotheque/interdisciplinary-art-research-7fb22bf05738), another on [Cultural Software](https://medium.com/historiotheque/cultural-software-97c169be1396), and finally, amongst countless other series of documents, [The Seasons of The Heart Liturgical Song-Cycle](https://medium.com/historiotheque/the-seasons-of-the-heart-liturgical-song-cycle-03c836c12103) and related works of art-research. - I at the very least need to slow down before I burn up and burn out;
- One of the main developments in the previous several months has been research on a new science — after *Historiomics —* called **Historionautics**; *Historionautics* is the interdisciplinary science and art of navigating history — both as a physical and conceptual space — by employing sophisticated systems of design, construction, mathematics, and mechanics. It represents a fusion of historiography, data science, cultural analysis, and systems theory, providing the tools and frameworks necessary to navigate the vast, multidimensional network of historical events, processes, and structures. In *Historionautics*, history is conceptualized as a spatial, navigable environment composed of structures, flows, and networks. Navigating this space requires sophisticated tools that are similar in design to those used in aerospace or maritime navigation but adapted to the *cultural, temporal, and structural dynamics* of history itself.
