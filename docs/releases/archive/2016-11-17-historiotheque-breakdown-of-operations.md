# Historiotheque Breakdown of Operations

*By J.G. Dufray · Historiotheque · Nov 17, 2016 · 2 min read*
*Source: https://medium.com/historiotheque/historiotheque-breakdown-of-operations-da8f921bedae*
*Converted to Markdown 2026-10-02. Images carried over as links to Medium's CDN.*

The Historiotheque v.2.0.2 spiralled out of control. It was a complete failure. We will try to explain but it's going to be very hard. We lost control of almost everything. The place is in ruins, so to speak. Thank goodness for the modularity we baked into it. It’s relatively easy to get back up and running.

We just realized that as we scaled, the documentation and logging and everything just quickly stopped making any sense to anyone. That means that until we figure out what we’re going to do to make the documentation and logging processes simpler and easier to maintain we’re keeping the Historiotheque Operation down. This is effective immediately and and it will remain like this until further notice.

It’s a long story, but in October, we had the door open and there was a big gust of wind and then everything just ended up going wrong, one thing after another. Now we no longer know where anything is. That’s okay because the Refcards-System was designed to be robust in the face of this sort of thing.
