# The Historiotheque v. 2.0.1

*By J.G. Dufray · Historiotheque · Aug 20, 2016 · 2 min read*
*Source: https://medium.com/historiotheque/the-historiotheque-v-2-0-1-f571cd64f852*
*Converted to Markdown 2026-10-02. The original contains no images or embedded videos.*

The Historiotheque is the name of my art studio, the modular workspace that houses my “Art Operations”. It is a work-in-progress that is already 20+ years in the making. I am officially releasing version 2.0.1 today.

*What makes The Historiotheque unique? Why bother giving your art studio a fancy name in any case? What’s it like in there anyway? Why do you feel you need to write about your studio?*

Great set of mock questions. I’m glad you asked, imaginary being. First and foremost, what has become The Historiotheque is much more of a VISION if you will of “The Perfect Workspace” for a contemporary interdisciplinary artist. So in that regard, the actual workspace is only ever going to be just the next “release” or “version” of the VISION of “The Historiotheque”.

Versions between version 1.0 and the current release most all happened at another location. Also, when I say that it houses my “Art Operations”, technically we are speaking of Art Operations version 5.0.1. The many previous versions of that happened across 13 different locations, spanning a period of 20+ years. And those are just the “official” releases.

All will be properly documented in time. This is a work-in-progress and a work that has taken a great deal of time and will continue to take a great deal of time. Today just marks the official release of version 2.0.1. Technically speaking, version 2.0.0 of The Historioteque came out earlier this year, around the end of April, officially in the beginning of May. So it’s already taken a couple months to get up and running in the new location, so today just marks the 3-month anniversary, give or take, of version 2.0.0.

More information to be provided shortly. Thanks for being a kind audience.

Any questions?

*N.B.: Corrections were made to this document. I got confused between semantic versions of different projects being worked on within the same space. I need to keep the different projects separated, at least in their versioning system, so now that I see a huge flaw in semantic versioning, I will likely have to build a more modular and versatile inter-project versioning system.*
