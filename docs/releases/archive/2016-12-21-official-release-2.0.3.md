The Aftermath of the Autodestruction of version 2.0.2.

If you have been following the ups and downs of The Historiothèque, you will remember that version 2.0.2 of [the Historiothèque came to a rapid, disastrous end](https://medium.com/historiotheque/historiotheque-breakdown-of-operations-da8f921bedae). All was shut down for analysis. Now v.2.0.3 if being released.

The main accomplishment of version 2.0.3 of the Historiothèque is that it has become almost completely Platform-Agnostic, Medium-Agnostic, etc. In short, I have refined the methodology at this point to make it almost indestructible. I will still be working on further refinements, and will be posted regular updates about the Historiothèque in general.

What happened is that after the most recent “breakdown of operations”, everything has been properly analyzed and corrections have been made.

Version 2.0.3 of the Historiothèque has accomplished the impossible in many ways. The workspace has been honed down. It truly is beginning to live up to its name as a “modular workspace”. Usually, during the day the workspace, workshop, Atelier, art studio etc., functions as exactly that, an Atlier/Art Studio. At night, it becomes a recording studio, or what I call a Sound Design Laboratory.

The vision of of The Historiothèque has also evolved. See: [The True Vision of The Historiotheque](https://medium.com/historiotheque/the-true-vision-of-the-historiotheque-6b9d15ac91e7).

In essence, it is being designed at this point as a kind of “Sublime Warehouse”, a kind of private, personal Freeport, though we are far away from the ideal instance of [The Perfect Workspace](https://medium.com/the-painters-almanach/the-perfect-workspace-dce2d3314d7c).
