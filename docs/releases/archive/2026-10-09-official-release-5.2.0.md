*What has been happening in The Historiotheque labs since the last update*

**I**

This is the *Official Release of The Historiotheque, semantic version 5.2.0* — a MINOR version, and a big one. Everything in it is backward-compatible; nothing has been broken. After the clean break of 5.0.0 last May, which rebuilt the infrastructure from the ground up, this release adds features on top of that new architecture and streamlines the whole operation.

The state of the operation: running. The summer went the way my summers go — a hiatus, then a fallow stretch, production continuing daily at lower intensity — and September brought the relaunch, more productive than ever. A few more paintings were added to The Chronotopium Series, which was introduced in the last release. We are now in the Autumn of Joyful Being.

**II**

The idea of this minor release is simple: I took *The Historiotheque* and formalized it even more. I streamlined it and made it more efficient. I put it into repositories, created an organization on GitHub and a community on Zenodo, and did the whole Verifiability Stack. Concretely:

- **Governance and formalization.** A governance folder — the first constitutional layer of the operation: mission, vision, a statement of principles, ethics documents. The studio log spec went through two mintings: the latest two with v1.6 going live on September 30, superseded by v1.7 on October 4, with a Studio Log Vocabulary of 14 terms for the temperature of the workspace. Workspace Theory: Axioms and Consequences, v1.0.0. The Formalization of the Work-System begun, module by module. A doctrine now governs the versions themselves: a version is assigned at publication, not during drafting.

- **Instruments and archives.** The Official Releases of the past ten years are now preserved in full text: the complete archive, 19 documents from 2016 to 2026, converted to Markdown with their images and videos restored, under a new Official Release spec. The Declarations archive is live on GitHub. The Laboratory Notebook has been revived (Historiotheque Series #001). Refcards and Research Questions continue in strict sequence.

- **Repos and infrastructure.** *The Historiotheque GitHub organization* is in daily use — declarations, statements, studio logs, refcards, video discourses, governance. The site historiotheque.github.io is live, with historiotheque.ca as its custom domain. Commit signing is in place. The local-clone workflow on GitHub Desktop is now the standard way of working — the Cultural DevOps turn, as I have called it in passing.

- **Research.** The Cubism audit continues as a research program — its video is “WHAT IS CUBISM? An Art Operation, 1907–1914” (September 23, 2026). Historiophany of the first kind — the Cézanne realization — opened a new line in September. The Historiomics formalization corpus has been assembled and read in full. Noise Fields continues, in Affinity now — a new painting, *“AUTUMN OF ATONEMENT”*, this month. I am also working on several new paintings in the Historiotheque Series — one near-complete, the others unfinished for the time being. I am studying them carefully, and will continue to study them and add layers of paint, and maybe some collage work. I have also rewritten my 2025 piece *“[*Assisted by Machine Intelligence]”* for the agentic era; it appears on my own Medium and Substack accounts alongside this release.

- **Writings.** *“The Freeport of St-Hilaire”* (short story), *“Abstract Characterology”* (a book on my character-development method), and the novel *“The Chronotopium: A Story of Algorithmogenesis”* are all in progress. I have also worked hard on the *ExperimentalNovel* repository as a whole — on a novel called *“The Nihilist”*, on a novel called *“Historiophany”*, and on several older novels I never finished. And I found a flaw in my novel-writing enterprise: I can come up with fully-formed design concepts for whole 1000-page novels in the blink of an eye, but the work of actually writing them out has a huge bottleneck. The solution is to just write one page at a time, and slowly add pages to novels, instead of trying to write 1000 pages all at once — which is infeasible, especially when you have a dozen novels in various states of progress.

- **Strategy.** Bluesky is the main hub; YouTube carries everything sonic and the video discourses. A self-directed PhD-equivalent cursus is outlined, with the stylometric analysis of my own prose across twenty years as its flagship goal, amongst other related analyses.

**III**

One piece of bookkeeping closes with this release: the versions of the Art Operation itself.

The Art Operation existed before The Historiotheque. When I launched The Historiotheque at v.2.0.1 in August 2016, I wrote: *“when I say that it houses my ‘Art Operations’, technically we are speaking of Art Operations version 5.0.1. The many previous versions of that happened across 13 different locations, spanning a period of 20+ years.”* A month later, in the Abject Failure document: *“Art Operations housed within the Historiotheque were at v.5.0.1 at the beginning of the day and earlier this morning, before the failure of the Historiotheque, we unofficially released Art Operations v.5.0.2.”*

In May 2024, in the 4.2.0 release, I stated the distinction as plainly as I ever have: *“There is a difference between The Art Operation AND The Historiotheque… The Art Operation being more the business operation and the Historiotheque more the location or space which the Operation inhabits.”* That release ran *“a new MAJOR version, v.6.0.0”* of The Art Operation alongside v.4.2.0 of The Historiotheque. In December 2024 I joked that The Art Operation *“is not officially at v.6.0.1”*, and in September 2025 I wrote that it *“should technically be at minor version 6.0.1”*, the changes since having been patches.

The truth is that the Art Operation’s numbers were never one clean ladder. Version 5.0.1 in 2016; then 4.0 in March 2023; 5.0 later in 2023; 6.0.0 in 2024. Somewhere along the way the count accumulated errors — accounting errors, transcription errors, operator errors, in short. The dual versioning served its purpose when the operation and the place needed separate books. They no longer do: everything now runs through the streamlined Historiotheque.

So: The Art Operation is hereby given the symbolic version 7.0.0 — a fresh start, and its final version. From now on, there is one version track only: the Official Releases of The Historiotheque. (The repositories, for their part, each have their own releases and version numbers — *ExperimentalNovel v2.0.0*, for example — which are a separate type of release, and are not to be confused with the Official Releases of the practice.)

**IV**

Follow-through, as always — what was predicted, and what happened:

- In 4.3.1 (April 2025) I declared that none of my future releases would ever break backward compatibility. In May 2026, 5.0.0 broke it deliberately, because it had to. This release restores the backward-compatible line — on the new architecture, not the old one.

- In the shutdown notice of June 7, 2025, I promised to properly document everything and make it public. That promise is now kept in full: the complete full-text archive of the Official Releases stands converted and published.

- In the Quick Update of September 30, 2025, I anticipated a version 4.4.1, and possibly a MAJOR version. The 4.4.1 never deployed; the MAJOR arrived as 5.0.0.

- In 5.0.0 I promised continued Important Updates and more actual textual content. 5.1.0 followed on July 3, 2026, and this release continues the line.

**V**

What’s next:

- I will go over the last *Official Declaration of Production-Year*. No Important Update is needed: the Declaration I published in May is one year ahead — it covers not just this production year but the one after — so the work is review, not revision.

- *The Deep-Archives Project* moves forward — a whole new project, much more than a repair: I am correcting fatal flaws in my system (new hard drives, a couple of terabytes of space, and everything is going onto them), and the project will carry its own scripts for data analysis and data visualizations based on the contents of my digital archives.

- A lot of programming, mostly procedural generation and procedural texture — texture synthesis and the like: the Refcard system is on the go, and text engines — generative text-manipulation scripts for *The Experimental Novel* project.

- Reader summaries for the *Releases* archive will be decided and written. The reader summaries are a condensed version of the same release (like for this *Release*): just its main points, for someone browsing the repo who doesn’t want to read the full text. These live one level up, in `releases/` itself — *same filename* as the full text; the folder tells you which form you're reading.

- The Formalization of the Work-System continues, module by module; the Historiomics formalization will be joined to the scale of the individual art operation; the Cubism audit continues.

What is not done: the reader summaries are not yet written. The reconstruction note in the archive README is still provisional wording — I am still working on that. And a Retrospective — I did a Spring Retrospective last time, and I mean to do those periodically — is planned, but not yet written.
