I said in my last update that “v.3.0.3 of The Historiotheque is in standby for now”. I had just had the *Official Release v.3.0.2*. This was back on March 5th of 2023. Before that, I basically went on a 6-year sabbatical from posting updates to/new versions of The Historiotheque (Official Release v.3.0.1 was released on December 29th, 2016).

I’m happy to say that after the hyper-productive period around March of this year, I ended up “closing down” The Historiotheque, as predicted. I then “reopened” it and had it running again, in *Official Release v.3.0.3,* for another couple of weeks. Now I am about the close it down again, for now.

I created several new projects this time around, as I did back in the spring season of this year. I have super-intensive periods of creativity where I finish or work on many previous projects, “living out of the Archives”, as well as creating and working on many new projects. Then I have periods where things slow down more and I am less productive. But when I’m in a productive period, I am extremely prolific.

It’s unbelievable the amount of work I can create in a productive period when I “relaunch” The Historiotheque with a new version. We are nearing the launching of The Historiotheque *Official Release v.4.0.1,* which will be the next major update in terms of *Official Releases.*

Every year around the fall, for the last 9 years, I’ve released an *Official Declaration of Production-Year*, which includes *“forward-looking statements for potential future Stakeholders”* about my interdisciplinary artistic and research practice for the coming *Production-Year*, which is always one year in advance. This year, I started my *Official Declaration* early, on March 18th, 2023, which was the [*Official Declaration of Production-Year 2024–2025*](https://medium.com/the-painters-almanach/official-declaration-of-production-year-2024-2025-409de041379)*.*

I am now working on updates, and will have a much more elaborate update in the fall, when I usually release my *Official Declaration.* I’m almost at the point of releasing the *Official Declaration of Production-Year 2025–2026,* which would be an extremely revolutionary event.

I always release an *Official Declaration* for the following full-year starting in the fall. I was born in the fall and always produce my declarations then. Basically, since we are in 2023 right now and the declaration comes in 2023, its “*forward looking statements”* are for the following year, therefore this year it’s for the year 2024. I make the declarations for two years, though.

I do this precisely because they are *“forward-looking statements for potential future Stakeholders”.* The idea is that I am essentially making my future artistic practice *“predictable”.* I want potential future stakeholders to have a *“sure value”*, not something unpredictable. I want them to know exactly what projects I will be working on, what concepts and conceptual systems will underlie these projects, that sort of thing.

I am happy to say that for 9 straight years, I have always followed my declarations/predictions STRICTLY, and even exceeded them prolifically. In any case, this is the *Official Release v.3.0.3.* and I am closing up shop until the fall when I will release the *Official Release v.3.0.4.*

I haven’t been very diligent in writing articles on Medium on this account. I have another account called for [A.G.](https://medium.com/u/e9228e0cb627?source=post_page---user_mention--6f0f3a74fe88-----------------------------------------), called ***antisignal***. The Historiotheque also has a Twitter account at [***historiotheque***](https://twitter.com/historiotheque), where I publish these updates and other things related to my art-studio/art-labs, called The Historiotheque. I’m also working on a new website with the same name which will likely also be published in the fall of 2023.

With that, I bid thee farewell. Until we next meet! Expect much fruit to be borne between now and then, which will be my pleasure to share with you.
