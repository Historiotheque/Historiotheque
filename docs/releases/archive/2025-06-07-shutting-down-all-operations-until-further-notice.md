*How I’m Closing Shop and Taking Most of The Summer Off For My Psychospiritual Development*

![“VEIL OF CONTEMPLATION”. Abstract digital painting by A.G. © 2025. All Rights Reserved.](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*6LOGhi9vIXvrU5oywA5wQg.png)

**I**
As it says in the title and subtitle, I am shutting down all operations until further notice. It’s not because anything went wrong. In fact, it’s because everything went right! I have sustained an extremely high level of productivity now for 7 full months, longer I think than any real period of production in the past. I usually take winters off, sometimes other months across other seasons. I tend to shut down operations soon after I open up shop again, then shut down and relaunch again on a regular basis.

This time, however, I sustained this super-prolificness for 7 months. I think I haven’t been that productive and creative ever, as I was saying. Now I am taking the time to properly document everything so that I can have it as a reference and also so that I can make everything public. I am after all an *Open Source Artist.* I share my work for free on the web, which is very important to me.

I don’t believe in the *mystique* that is usually associated with artists. I also don’t believe in the *secrecy* of methods in creating artworks. I think that it is time that artists start sharing all of the processes involved in creating their artwork so that in essence the “experiments” can become *reproducible.* I think that this is of utmost importance. Increasingly, people are either becoming artists or beginning to explore the arts, dabbling in them if you will. I think that artists who do this professionally should set things up so that they document everything in their practices and making it freely available online so that anyone with a web browser can access it.

Artists can still make money with their craft. The two, sharing freely and commercializing the product, are not mutually exclusive. One can do both. I also believe in producing documentation that contains what I call *“forward-looking statements for potential future Stakeholders*” so that one can make one’s work *dependable.*

![“PORTRAIT OF A YOUNG WOMAN”. Abstract digital painting by A.G. © 2025. All Rights Reserved.](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*IcTKal_QJ4JM4dE1UZoOTA.png)

**II**
This past year has been very fruitful and I am quite happy about it. I am doing better and better as an individual and things are really starting to shape up. I like the ideas of *fruitfulness* and *fertility*, of ideas, of works, of life itself. There’s nothing like running a mature art studio and research lab, giving it all you’ve got, and succeeding in going beyond one’s wildest dreams and expectations. It is really what is called a *tour de force* in French.

In any case, I am shutting down all operations until further notice, as stated above. I will take most of the summer to spending time walking outdoors, exploring the great landscape of the region where I live, the mountain, the village, and the river and I will also be spending time with family and friends, enjoying one another’s company. The summer is a great time to take time off. The weather is nice and it’s just a season of joy.

Last winter, for me, was a *“Winter of Joyful Being”* which was now followed by a *“Springtime of Joyful Being”.* Usually my winters, though, are *“Winters of The Soul”*, but it wasn’t this time. I will now have a *“Summer of The Heart”*, followed later on this year by an *“Autumn of Atonement”*, or possibly an *“Autumn of Joyful Being”* (I like these *“Seasons of Joyful Being”* as a theme!*).*

That’s about it for now. In short, after a seriously productive and lengthy season of production, I am taking a break until further notice. That’s about all I had to say for today. Peace be with you and I’ll see you on the other side! I always reserve the right to come back to this document and add what I like to call *“IMPORTANT UPDATES”.* Thanks for your time and attention, it means a great deal to me.

Before I go, I want to add that what I am essentially closing down is *The Historiotheque Semantic Version 4.3.1.,* which was a *MINOR VERSION (*[***Official Release of The Historiotheque Semantic Version 4.3.1. (MINOR VERSION)***](https://medium.com/historiotheque/official-release-of-the-historiotheque-semantic-version-4-3-1-minor-version-d6c321aaee82)*).* The next update may be a minor version or a major version, or else just a *PATCH VERSION*. It all depends on the process of going over all the work I’ve just done and documenting all of the processes involved in the practice and work. After a long analysis, we should have a new version of the *Cultural Software knows as The Historiotheque.* Thanks again!
