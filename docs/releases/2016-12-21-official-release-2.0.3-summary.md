# Official Release — Historiothèque v.2.0.3 — summary

Version 2.0.3 is released on December 21, 2016, in the aftermath of the "breakdown of operations" that brought v2.0.2 to a rapid, disastrous end. Everything was shut down, properly analyzed, and corrections were made.

The main accomplishment of v2.0.3 is that The Historiothèque has become almost completely Platform-Agnostic, Medium-Agnostic, etc. — the methodology refined to the point of being almost indestructible, with further refinements to come.

The workspace has been honed down and is beginning to live up to its name as a "modular workspace": during the day it functions as an Atelier/Art Studio; at night it becomes a recording studio — a Sound Design Laboratory. The vision has also evolved: The Historiothèque is being designed as a kind of "Sublime Warehouse," a private, personal Freeport, though still far from the ideal instance of The Perfect Workspace.
