# The Latest Official Release of The Historiotheque Semantic Version 4.2.0 — summary

May 23, 2024. A new MINOR version of the Cultural Software known as The Historiotheque — changes that add functionality in a backward-compatible manner. At the same time, The Art Operation is running a new MAJOR version, ArtOps semantic version 6.0.0. The distinction is restated: The Art Operation is more the business operation; The Historiotheque is more the location or space the Operation inhabits; both are run as Cultural Software, each its own AntiOS — an operating system for organizing and reorganizing a modular workspace of "variable geometry."

The release recaps May 2024: May 1, the preliminary launching of v3.0.5 after a fallow period; May 4, the immediate Official Release of v4.0.1; May 7, an IMPORTANT UPDATE on The New Documentation (an update, not a release of any version). The rapid starting-up and shutting-down is explained by the BABEL UP / BABEL DOWN process, quoted from March 2023: stay too long in an active Art Operation and disorder and disorganization peak at unsustainable levels — you shut down THE WHOLE OPERATION, then scale back up.

Over the three weeks before this release, activity became NON-SUSTAINABLE; v4.2.0 was written to make major fixes that stayed compatible with v4.0.1. Productivity is reported at A HISTORIC PEAK, the whole Operation significantly more streamlined and efficient — as optimized as is humanly possible, for the time being, for a "One-Person Operation." The fixes named are mostly matters of daily structure: the "morning constitutional" walk resumed, days better structured and time better managed, dozens of books read, a great deal written to good feedback, a new GitHub repository "Cultural Software" created, and D.I.D.I. conceived — The Distributed International Design Initiative, a much larger-scale vision of "Historiotheques Distributed All Across The Land."

Closing position: not officially shutting down The Art Operation, but officially "on hiatus" until further notice — art and research continue — with the rest of the summer and then the spring given mostly to walking, reading, writing, and time with those closest, and the entire MEDIA STRATEGY under rework.

The release closes with an extended statement of the Cultural Software concept (dated May 5, 2024): Cultural Software operating in Cultural Software Space, guided by the principles of Historiomics and "The Metaphysics of Art Movements," with the AntiOS encoding and executing algorithmic representations of cultural software within Creative Work-Systems; moraltempcards — historiotypes — as mechanisms for encoding and preserving the moral temperature of historical moments; and the whole aimed at the faithful transmission of the cultural treasure across generations.
