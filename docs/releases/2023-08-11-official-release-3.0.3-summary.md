# The Historiotheque v.3.0.3 — summary

This release follows Official Release v3.0.2 (March 5, 2023), which ended a roughly 6-year sabbatical from posting updates — v3.0.1 had been released on December 29, 2016. After the hyper-productive period around March 2023, The Historiotheque closed down as predicted, then reopened as v3.0.3 for another couple of weeks. With this release (August 11, 2023) it closes down again, until the fall.

The release states the working rhythm plainly: super-intensive periods of creativity — finishing or advancing many previous projects, "living out of the Archives," and creating new ones — alternate with periods where things slow down. Several new projects were created this time around, as in the spring. The next major update in terms of Official Releases is expected to be v4.0.1.

It also sets out the declarations cycle: for 9 years, an Official Declaration of Production-Year has been released every fall — "forward-looking statements for potential future Stakeholders" for the coming Production-Year, made one year in advance and covering two years, so the practice stays "predictable" for stakeholders. The declarations are reported as followed strictly, and even exceeded, for 9 straight years. The fall update will be more elaborate, alongside Official Release v3.0.4; work on the Official Declaration of Production-Year 2025–2026 is under way.
