# The Rebirth of The Historiotheque — summary

The previous release, v3.0.1 (December 29, 2016), was followed by a sabbatical meant to last a year; it lasted six. With this release (March 5, 2023) The Historiotheque is up and running again, as Official Release v3.0.2.

The release records the turn: after several years of producing less and less in the Art Operation ("ArtOps 2.0"), a hugely prolific period — new cultural artifacts posted across his platforms and blogs, and the operator back on track, more inspired and structured than in many years. The work's centre of gravity at the relaunch: music and interdisciplinary research, and programming in Python — the Refcards-System, and Schizobot, the archival system and production methodology meant to function as an intelligent agent. Short texts accompanied by digital works and photography; audio recordings in the field and in the studio, and video content, to come.

It also restates the ground the operation stands on: the work proceeds in three mediums — IMAGE / SOUND / TEXT — as it has since The History-Project began in the summer of 2001; the operator works as a Field Artist ("Artiste de Terrain"), an experimental researcher and producer; and the ALX system, his rigorous design workflow management methodology, is to be worked on. "The Art Operation 3.0" is adopted as the working methodology. More to come.
