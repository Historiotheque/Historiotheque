# A Summer of The Heart: A Displaced Fallow Period (v4.2.1) — summary

December 14, 2024. The Art Operation has been in an intense fallow period since about June 2024 — almost seven months, a "cold summer" — and The Historiotheque is now up and running again. Version 4.2.1 is technically a patch: a backward-compatible bug fix, with no real extra functionality added.

The bug in question was a distinct passivity in the slow period of relative inaction — though projects were still worked on, badly needed reading done, and writing kept up every day. Building slowly toward greater mobilization turned up an anomaly: **A Summer of The Heart Acting as A Winter of The Soul.** There are many types of seasons; each has its own liturgical expressions across three disciplines or "modes" — Image(s), Sound(s), and Text(s) — forming the secular liturgy of The Seasons of The Heart song-cycle and the paintings and writings that go with it.

Follow-through on v4.2.0 (May 23, 2024): the hiatus announced there was kept — producing art and research throughout, while walking, reading, writing, and reworking the entire MEDIA STRATEGY. The fallow period itself carried a bug, a "noise-bit," which created "logopathic" processes echoing through the period — a kind of "personal historiopathy." Work continues on the Creative Work-System ALX and the documentation series: Database Arts, The Refcards-Project, The New Documentation, The AntiOS Concept, The Project-Based Serial Method, The Switchboard Method, The Art-Ops-In-A-Box Concept, Noise in The Workspace, Foundation Models, Cultural Software, and The Seasons of The Heart Liturgical Song-Cycle.

**IMPORTANT UPDATE (December 14, 10h44):** Seven hours after launch, another bug has slipped in — this one on the side of "too intense" creative activity and productivity — and a SHUT DOWN OF OPERATIONS UNTIL FURTHER NOTICE is already being considered; at the very least, a slowdown before burning up and burning out. The update reports a main development of the previous months: research on a new science, after Historiomics, called **Historionautics** — the interdisciplinary science and art of navigating history, both as a physical and conceptual space, history conceptualized as a spatial, navigable environment of structures, flows, and networks, with tools comparable to aerospace or maritime navigation adapted to history itself. Also: work on "The Archives-Project: The Finale," and the revived "Historiomic Hypervisor" — a design concept for a user interface for "history-machines," historical navigation systems for navigating Historiomes, shown as a paper prototype.

**IMPORTANT UPDATE (December 15, 04h45):** After an extremely intense 24-hour period of production, the Art Operation is placed basically on hiatus on official terms until further notice — art and research continue — the natural oscillation of relaunch, raise-up, and shut-down restated. Also recorded: an IMPORTANT UPDATE on the Official Declaration of Production-Year 2026–2027, a video presentation on Official Declarations, The Painter's Almanach, and the Seasons of The Heart Project, and a new blog piece assisted by machine intelligence, "Omniscopics: The Meta-Architecture of Reality and Being."
