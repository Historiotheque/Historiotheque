# Official Release: The Historiotheque version 2.0.2 — summary

The workspace is back online. After a little over a day of operations, everything was shut down for data analysis, then rebuilt from scratch over the following four days. Relaunch is set for Monday, the 19th of September, 2016.

Maybe a week of work was lost, but the operation is better positioned than before. Until any bugs with the current release v2.0.2 of The Historiotheque are found and fixed, no new assignments are being taken in.

Work in progress in the meantime includes the official Declaration for Autumn 2017, for Production-Year 2018–2019, among a few other things, until further notice.
