# The Preliminary Launching of The Historiotheque Semantic Version 3.0.5 — summary

Version 3.0.4 closed down near the end of November 2023, followed as predicted by a "fallow period" — a "Winter of The Soul." On May 1, 2024, v3.0.5 is preliminarily launched: the operator reports feeling revitalized, and predicts the Spring and Summer "Seasons of The Heart" will be extremely productive.

The release recaps the declarations feat of 2023: the Official Declaration of Production-Year 2024–2025 was produced early, in March 2023, and then the Declaration covering 2025–2026 came out at the end of August 2023 — a full year in advance, an unprecedented event in the practice. Work continues on "DISMANTLING THE ARCHIVE," on the Historiotheque website, and on the Creative Work-System ALX. Projects named include Database Arts, The Refcards-Project, The Stacks-Project, The New Documentation, The AntiOS Concept, The Project-Based Serial Method, The Switchboard Method, The Art-Ops-In-A-Box Concept, Noise in The Workspace, Foundation Models, Cultural Software, The Seasons of The Heart Liturgical Song-Cycle, and Workspace Management.

The release repeats the throttling doctrine: a sustainable, mature practice requires carefully throttling activity — too much, for too long, ends in burn-out.

**IMPORTANT UPDATE (May 3, 2024):** v3.0.5 has officially been TOTALLY SHUT DOWN, two days after its preliminary launch. The update calls it the most prolific period of the operator's life: some 36 projects juggled in continuous delivery, works published across three disciplines in 10 separate places on the web, more books read in 4–5 days than ever before. But it started too fast: too much sleep lost, a Critical State reached, the pace wholly Non-Sustainable. The operation goes on HIATUS until further notice, and the next version of The Historiotheque is expected to be Semantic Version 4.0.1.
