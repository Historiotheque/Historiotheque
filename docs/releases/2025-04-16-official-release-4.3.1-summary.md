# Official Release of The Historiotheque Semantic Version 4.3.1 (MINOR VERSION) — summary

April 16, 2025. A correction to the framing of v4.2.1 (December 2024): what was reported there as a fallow period gave way, in truth, to a new, truly productive season beginning in November 2024 — so productive that a shutdown and sabbatical seemed likely. That didn't happen. Instead, a new MINOR version. The winter was probably the most creative and productive season EVER in the practice — a "Winter of Joyful Being" — and the season, the longest yet at such a level, has run some five months.

This was almost the release of a MAJOR version: the entire Art Operation was essentially revamped, but kept backward-compatible — and future releases, it is stated, will always be backward-compatible. There was a partial shutdown in January into part of February 2025, but the operation was kept going; the practice is being implemented as continuous delivery, with the documentation catching up after the busiest stretch. All lingering bugs in The Historiotheque OS are reported fixed, the system so streamlined that it may now run full-time, without the shutdowns each "Production-Season" that these Official Releases have chronicled.

The season's production: abstract digital paintings with speculative poetic prose, daily or near-daily since November; hundreds of pages of philosophical notes; the video discourse "OFFICIAL DECLARATIONS, THE PAINTER'S ALMANACH, AND THE SEASONS OF THE HEART PROJECT"; and a chain of new concepts, developed with the assistance of machine intelligence:

- **Historionautics** (September 2024) — the science and art of navigating history, built on nonlinear historiography: history not as a linear timeline but as a complex, interconnected web of events, themes, and influences; navigation as a practical, explorative approach to it.
- **Historiotronics** — the higher-order theory extending Historionautics: history as a dynamic, multi-dimensional system of interconnected networks, encompassing the past, speculative futures, parallel narratives, and alternate possibilities; a formalized, systems-oriented approach.
- **Chronotronics** — the system synthesizing and transcending Historiomics, Historionautics, and Historiotronics: a universal framework for engaging with the structures, processes, and flows of time, history positioned as one dimension of a broader chronosystem.
- **HISTORIOPHANY** (early 2025) — called possibly the most significant breakthrough of the operator's life. A historiophany is a "historical epiphany": the dynamic, experiential, and often transformative moment when history is revealed — the "phenomenological unveiling" of history as a living, felt presence, the past breaking into the present, as in hearing an old church bell and "being transported in time." Alongside it stands The Historiophant, the figure who interprets history. Historiophany is presented as the apotheosis or culmination-point of History.

These concepts will go into future chapters of the novel The Archives-Project (in progress for at least ten years, sequel to The History-Project). Out of the novel's fictional project also comes The HistoriOS System: a continuous, dynamic, machine-intelligence-sustained global infrastructure for the immersive curation, simulation, and phenomenological navigation of historical and historiophanic data — an infinite exhibition environment navigated by historionauts, in which sillage, the lingering trace of history (a term from perfumery), constitutes both the mode and the matter of access. Ahead: a "Springtime of Joyful Being" — most seasons going forward, it is thought, will be "Seasons of Joyful Being."
