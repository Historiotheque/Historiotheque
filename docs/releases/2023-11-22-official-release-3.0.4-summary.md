# Relaunch and Subsequent Closing Down of The Historiotheque Semantic Version 3.0.4 — summary

The pattern of 2023: the Art Operation went up in March and was closed down; went up again in July and was closed down after a few weeks; and now v3.0.4 (November 22, 2023) comes out after a few weeks of extremely intense activity — and is again being closed down.

Follow-through: as predicted in v3.0.3, the historiotheque.ca website was published — the domain was purchased in July 2023, and a one-page temporary site is up until a better-designed one can be built; future Official Releases are to be posted there.

The release explains the operation for new readers. The Historiotheque is the art studio, which also serves as a sound design laboratory, research space, recording studio, and living quarters. From formal studies of theoretical computer science and programming came the borrowing of software practice — Official Releases, semantic versioning, version control — applied to an interdisciplinary art-research practice: the Art Operation (ArtOps 5.0) housed in a MODULAR SPACE with "variable geometry," a concept worked on for at least fifteen years.

The method is the raise-up / bring-down cycle: "ArtBurst Episodes" of intense creative activity, then the practice brought down to a "simmer" — the "BABEL UP / BABEL DOWN" process — because such turbulence is not sustainable in the medium-to-long term. The work-system behind it is named ALX, a "rigorous design workflow management methodology"; alongside the Workspace stands the Lifespace, the two together called AntiOS, "The Anti-Operating System."

A new direction is announced: "DISMANTLING THE ARCHIVE" — a kind of 180-degree turn, expected to take years, after some 30 years of building up the personal archive. Sessions, periods, projects, series, and collections alike need clearly-delimited boundaries: START_SIGNALS, STOP_SIGNALS, "Terminus-Stones." Ahead lies the winter slow period, the "Long Tail of Analysis."
