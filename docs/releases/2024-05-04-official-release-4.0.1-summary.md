# Immediate, Official Release of The Historiotheque Semantic Version 4.0.1 — summary

May 4, 2024. This is the first MAJOR version of The Historiotheque since Semantic Version 3.0.1 (December 29, 2016), and it is INCOMPATIBLE with the previous MAJOR version — per Semantic Versioning: MAJOR for incompatible changes, MINOR for backward-compatible functionality, PATCH for backward-compatible bug fixes. The release owns a "jockey" error: only PATCH versions were ever released, never MINOR versions; this will be corrected moving forward.

Why 3.0.5 was shut down after 3 days: the goal of The Historiotheque Anti-Operating System, as fully operational "Cultural Software," is a mature, SUSTAINABLE interdisciplinary art-research practice — THE ART OPERATION. Version 3.0.5 contained important "modes of failure," major bugs/errors in how the operation was run, which brought real costs and risked losing the operational/business continuity of the practice — something that has never happened in 30 years. The bugs were patched and corrected, and v4.0.1 reports a fully sustainable practice, possibly for the first time in real and official terms in 30 years.

The release also gives the full account of the declarations system: in year X, an Official Declaration is always produced for years X+1 and X+2 — one Production-Year in advance, the Production-Year starting every Autumn — on the Design House model of releasing the "New Collection" one year ahead. The underlying concept governing the Production-Year is THE PAINTER'S ALMANACH. Ten declarations are listed, from Production-Year 2016–2017 through 2025–2026 (the last released a full year in advance, in August 2023; 2024–2025 ahead of schedule, in March 2023). The stated aim is a predictable practice for "potential future stakeholders" — dependable, though not entirely predictable, since accidents happen; the record claimed is 9 years of never failing to live up to the declarations.
