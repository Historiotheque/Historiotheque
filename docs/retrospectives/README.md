# Retrospectives

A register of the Retrospectives of the Art Operation @ The Historiotheque.

Retrospectives are reflection-first documents: considered looks back over a period of the work — a season, a year, a project — written after the fact. The day-to-day record lives in the studio logs; a Retrospective is where the work is weighed.

Past Retrospectives are scattered across texts, logs, and video presentations. This folder therefore keeps a register rather than an archive: each Retrospective is entered here with its title, date, and medium, and where it lives. Written Retrospectives are filed in this folder; Retrospectives in other media are registered here and kept where they are.
