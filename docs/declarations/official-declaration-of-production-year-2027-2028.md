**I
**It’s become commonplace at this point for me to say that I am publishing my _Official Declarations of Production-Year_ one or more years in advance, _Declarations_ that already cover years in advance. My goal is always the same: _To make my art and research practice as DEPENDABLE as possible._

Some of my previous _Official Declarations_ have been more about what I was currently doing in my practice than what I planned to be doing in the future, though they are essentially the same thing as I work on the same couple dozen projects more or less all the time. This year, I’m not going to go too much into my previous and current projects, but will really try to project myself into the future and make some statements about my future practice. I always reserve the right to come back and make corrections.

![“THROUGH THE CODE’S EMBRACE: A STUDY IN RESILIENCE”. Abstract digital painting by A.G. © 2025. All Rights Reserved.](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*ZOkV80zG8tBBBgCpmhjJXQ.png)

My art and research practice is increasingly becoming about _interdisciplinarity_ itself, its very nature. I mix three different artistic disciplines as well as a handful of fields of research, all with a single impetus and motivation. My goal is to help people develop a great appreciation of beauty and other aesthetic principles as well as experiences. I believe that beauty, peace, tranquility, silence, communion, and other related principles and states are fundamental to the human experience, in the universal sense of the _fulfilled, complete human being._

Also, I am increasingly mixing the analog with the digital, making a _hybrid creative expression._ Moreover, I am seeking inspiration more and more from the inward space of my heart and soul, going as deeply as I can into that seemingly endless and bottomless inner space.

I have been experimenting with a contemplative practice for about 15 years now, at least in a more formal manner than what I was doing before. I’ve always been a solitary individual, but now I’ve been practicing meditation, contemplation, and prayer in my day-to-day life. This contemplative practice is where I get most of my inspiration, again in that open space of inwardness. I am talking more about this aspect of my life in my social communications and it has been bearing fruit in unexpected ways.

**III
**I’m constantly reviewing my past work, from all of the different projects that I’ve worked on in the last 30 years of daily interdisciplinary art-research practice.

Something that I’ve been working on for the last 15 or so years that I want to talk about is something I call _“The Refcards-System”_ or _“Refcards-Project”_ at times. It is essentially a kind of simplified library card catalog but for _“Refcards”_ that treat of unique concepts, what I call _“atoms of knowledge”_. Its design was inspired by a few things, online library card catalogs, Paul Otlet’s _Mundaneum,_ and also two unique pieces of software, namely ZOOMRACKS and the HyperCard system. It’s also inspired by the World Wide Web. It’s basically a simplified Word Wide Web, running locally, that knowledge workers can use to create _“Cardboxes”_ that they can share with one another, similar to a bookmarking application.

I really haven’t invented anything new with _“The Refcards-System”,_ I just wrote a technical specification that is a unique blend of many source materials. In any case, I began experimenting with an app called Lovable which uses AI to design websites or applications or whatnot. You simply type what kind of app or website you want as a prompt and the AI designs and generates the actual app or website. I gave it my technical specification for _“The Refcards-System”_ and it created the mock-up shown below.

![“THE REFCARDS-SYSTEM”. Mock-up made with the app via Lovable.](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*A9ewZjJoLYHlI0gwc-2Q6w.png)

I have been learning to write code in the Python programming language for the last 12 or 13 years. I have worked on many projects, mostly simple scripts, though, to do various things on my computer. I want to keep learning the ins and outs of Python, though, and work on _“The Refcards-System”_ and other pieces of _“cultural software”_ as I like to call it.

I would be able to have something like _“The Refcards-System”_ to publish all of the documentation for my many projects. Each _“Refcard”_ would contain a single concept or aspect of the work, and cards could be connected with one another in a vast network of _“Refcards”._ The goal is to build knowledge bases in a minimalistic way, with a minimalistic software application.

_“The Refcards-Project”_ fits into a larger, overarching concept of what I call _“Database Arts”_, which is a whole aesthetic that is based on relational databases, their concepts and underlying theory. This is after all the age of the database. It’s only normal that artists begin emulating them, though they already have been for several decades. The works are put together in a vast database where individual works are in an unordered set. You can search through what is essentially a massive **_archive_**.

I like to work _“INTO THE ARCHIVE”_. I jump right into the archive and begin going over my old projects, my old artworks, using them to inspire new projects and artworks. I work OUT of the archive, as a base/database.

As I said, one of the big themes that my work up until 2028 is going to be _interdisciplinarity_, between the artistic disciplines of IMAGE(s), SOUND(s), and TEXT(s) and the many domains of knowledge I work across in my research. Other than that, anything I might have missed will be covered in future IMPORTANT UPDATES. Thanks for your time and attention and peace be with you!

I’ve mentioned elsewhere that I have been working on a new concept of “[historiophany](https://github.com/antiface/HISTORIOPHANY)” for quite a while now. I’ve also come up with a concept of “[historiomancy](https://github.com/antiface/HISTORIOPHANY/tree/main/HISTORIOMANCY)” as well as “[historiagogy](https://github.com/antiface/HISTORIOPHANY/tree/main/HISTORIAGOGY)” of late. Really, I’m coming down to the very essence of all these “historio-” terms that I’ve been coming up with in the last 20 or so years. Here are short definitions of each of these “historio-” terms:

**_General Theory of Historiophany_**

*   _The term historiophany can be etymologically derived from the Greek roots historia (narrative, inquiry, history) and phanein (to appear, to reveal, or to shine forth). Thus, historiophany can be understood as “the manifestation or revelation of history.” This term suggests a conceptual framework where history is not merely recorded or studied but experienced or revealed in a manner akin to an epiphany — a transformative encounter with the past in its most profound and revelatory form._

**_Concept of Historiophany_**

*   _Historiophany is the dynamic, experiential, and often transformative moment when history is revealed to individuals or collectives in a way that transcends the conventional study of historical facts. It is the “phenomenological unveiling” of history as a living, felt presence, where the past breaks into the present and is understood as shaping, informing, or challenging contemporary consciousness._

**_Historiomancy_** _(from Gk. ἱστορία + μαντεία) may be defined as:_

*   _A mode of divination that uses the study, presence, and residues of historical material — records, narratives, artifacts, ruins, testimonies, traditions, and absences — as an oracular medium for perceiving, interpreting, or foretelling latent or emergent truths within temporal human experience._
*   _This implies that history, in this context, is not simply chronological narrative or archival record, but a symbolic and semiotic substrate, potentially alive with meaning that exceeds conventional historiographical interpretation._

**_Historiagogy_**

*   _The site of practice (archive, city, studio, digital network, collaborative system) becomes a historical field-structure. The participants become historical agents embedded in temporal strata. The sequence of actions becomes a procedural unfolding of historical intelligibility._
*   _Thus, historiagogy is not the study of history as object, but the orchestration of conditions under which history manifests itself._

Something else that is going to becoming increasingly important in my work is my software project I have been calling “[Schizobot](https://github.com/antiface/Schizobot)”, which is what I tentatively have been calling “The Stupidest System in The Universe”. It is a reproduction in a way that I’ve been able to produce, of a schizophrenic artificial intelligence. Here is a quick definition of the Schizobot system:

**_Overview of the Schizobot System_**

*   _The Schizobot system can be conceptualized as an abstract computational architecture in theoretical computer science, akin to a modular, hierarchical framework of interconnected abstract classes and interfaces. It models a simulated artificial intelligence ecosystem inspired by schizophrenic cognition, moral philosophy, creativity, and adversarial dynamics. At its core, Schizobot operates as a multi-agent system where modules function as polymorphic entities — each an abstraction encapsulating state, behavior, and interactions — engaging in non-deterministic, emergent computations. These modules interact via implicit “import” dependencies (resembling mixin compositions or trait inheritance), event-driven signaling, and shared state spaces, forming a complex network that simulates disorganized, paranoid, and creative cognitive processes._
